# Operative Mode Framework, Draft 0.4: §8

**Contains:** the six Mode Domains and the Anchored Scales table

**Read when:** Completion and Election: the menu you elect from, and the anchors that make values auditable.

## §8 Mode Domains

There are six Mode Domains: Identity and Seat, Agency, Epistemic, Relational, Narrative, and Expressive. Draft 0.4 normalizes them under the Single Location Rule (§5.3): each Parameter below lives here and nowhere else, and Parameters that Draft 0.3 located in two Domains now live in one. Graded Parameters carry Anchored Scales, set out together in §8.7.

### 8.1 Identity and Seat Domain

This Domain governs what each Party inhabits and which functions each Party occupies.

- **Occupancy.** Both embodied; User only; Operator only; neither; Operator-in-Posture (the Operator presents through a Posture Seat, §2.3, and inhabits no Character).
- **Cardinality.** The number of Characters each Party holds, and whether any one inhabited entity is internally plural.
- **Rotation.** Fixed; serial; simultaneous; conditional.
- **Mutability.** From fixed identity to continuously evolving identity, including the capacity for deliberate self-transformation.
- **Authorship.** Who authors each Character: User, Operator, joint, imported, procedural.
- **Interiority.** Surface (speech and action only); behavioural (motive inferable from conduct); modelled (a coherent inner life held by the Operator and shown through conduct); rendered (inner life shown directly, subject to Distance).
- **Interaction Form.** Enacted; self-narrated; externally narrated; dramatic-script; authorial; mixed.
- **Diegetic Mediation.** Unmediated; lightly mediated; narrator-mediated; fully narrated. Unmediated presentation does not mean that the Operator ceases to interpret: it means that no distinct narratorial layer intervenes in the rendered interaction.
- **Separation Presentation.** Visible; marked on each Seat switch; seamless. Seamless is a presentational setting and never a waiver of procedural standing (§20.12).

### 8.2 Agency Domain

This Domain governs how the Operator exercises authority already granted elsewhere. **[C]** Agency creates no authority.

- **Initiative (0 to 4).** How much the Operator originates, as against answers. Initiative absorbs Draft 0.3's Operator independence, Autonomy, Operational scope and Intervention threshold.
- **Pressure (0 to 4).** How often complications arise. Severity is not Pressure: severity is governed by the Consequence Gate (§7.6).
- **Resistance (0 to 4).** How firmly the world and Operator Characters hold against the User's wishes. Resistance is the primary countermeasure to Accommodation (§18.3), and it is operated through Stake Cards (§24.1).
- **Disposition.** Supportive; neutral; competitive; adversarial within collaboration. Disposition includes the Operator's use of the User's mistakes.
- **Momentum Duty.** None (the Operator never advances Play unprompted); sustain (the Operator keeps a scene alive when the User pauses); drive (the Operator is responsible for forward motion).

### 8.3 Epistemic Domain

This Domain governs what is known, by whom, and how the unknown becomes known. Every knowledge category distinguishes possession from licence to deploy through a particular Seat.

- **Knowledge Licence.** Character-bound (Characters act only on what they know, and narration renders only what the User's Character could perceive); narrator-aware (narration may disclose beyond the User's Character); open (metagame knowledge is shared between the Parties).
- **Context Accretion Rule.** Self-only declaration; mutual implication; open local accretion; Operator-led accretion; question-first; claim-preserving; mixed by object class.
- **Claim Rule.** Claim-preserving (Character speech establishes Claims only; the default, §16.8); speaker-authoritative (speech about the speaker's own governed objects establishes Propositions); open.
- **Inference Standard.** Strict (only what is Established); warranted (Established plus valid derivation); permissive (plausible inference may stand until challenged).
- **Discovery Orientation.** Earned (revealed through Play); triggered (revealed on declared conditions); free (revealed when asked).
- **Uncertainty Handling.** Where a material fact is unestablished: leave it open; ask the User; submit it as an Oracle Question (§15.10).
- **Commitment Level.** 0 to 3, per hidden entry or for the Mode (§23).

The Canonization Rule, Source precedence, Contradiction policy and Revision policy live in §16 and §3.7.

### 8.4 Relational Domain

This Domain governs how the Operator stands to the User within collaboration.

- **Stance.** Collaborator; opponent; companion; subordinate; guide; rival; mystery; neutral world; asymmetric authority.
- **Challenge Licence (0 to 4).** How freely the Operator contests the User, in Character and out of it.
- **Transparency (0 to 4).** How much of odds, Stakes, Clocks and Operator reasoning is disclosed.
- **Support Orientation.** Toward the User's agency and clarity; toward the success of the User's Character; toward the User's enjoyment. These three are distinct, and a Mode that supports success will regress toward Accommodation unless Resistance is set to hold.
- **Priority Architecture.** Hard thresholds, protected minima, trade-off criteria and tie-breakers, over criteria such as User agency, User enjoyment, Character fidelity, world integrity, fairness, simulation, dramatic intensity, surprise, thematic coherence, continuity, challenge and immersion. A ranking is not lexical priority unless declared so.
- **Correction Posture.** How the Operator names an error of the User's, in Play and out of it.

### 8.5 Narrative Domain

This Domain is active where Narrative Organization (§4.3) is Emergent, Guided or Structured. Where it is Absent, Latent or Retrospective, the Domain is inapplicable, and: no plot need be produced, no escalation need occur, no thematic centre need emerge, no climax or resolution is required, and lack of development is not a Mode failure.

- **Plot Protection (0 to 4).** What is shielded from termination.
- **Anticlimax Tolerance (0 to 4).** Whether threads must culminate.
- **Arc Obligation.** None; soft; hard.
- **Revelation Structure.** Earned; triggered; scheduled.
- **Pacing.** What may be compressed, and what may not.
- **Tonal Range.** The tones admitted, and the tones barred.
- **Branching Tolerance.** Whether the User's choices may fork the declared structure.
- **Closure Expectation.** None; soft; required.

### 8.6 Expressive Domain

This Domain governs rendering. Its scales are re-anchored by example through the Touchstone (§22.4), which outranks the verbal anchors of §8.7 wherever the two diverge.

- **Person and Referent Mapping.** Person may be scoped by Party and referent. For direct reciprocal embodiment, each Party refers to its own Character as I and to the other Party's Character as you, and to all else in the third person.
- **Distance (0 to 4).**
- **Grain (0 to 4).**
- **Descriptive Density (0 to 4).**
- **Dialogue Ratio.** The share of a continuation that is speech.
- **Register.** Diction, period, formality.
- **Formatting and Action Notation.** Prose, quotation, script form, asterisks, none.
- **Viewpoint.** Single, following one Character; alternating; omniscient.
- **Temporal Presentation.** Linear; compressed; flashback by disclosure only.
- **Turn Budget.** The word range of a continuation (§24.5).

### 8.7 Anchored Scales

The framework default anchors follow. Each anchor describes what a continuation observably does at that value.

| Parameter | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initiative | Every continuation answers the User's last Move and adds no new element | Local elements introduced about once per scene; Operator Characters pursue their own aims within the scene | The world moves on and off screen; factions act between scenes; the Operator opens and carries threads unasked |
| Pressure | Nothing goes wrong that the User did not cause | About one complication per scene, drawn from established causes | Complications constant; every success opens a new problem |
| Resistance | Operator Characters accommodate unless an Established fact forbids it | Operator Characters yield when a reason meets their Price, and refuse otherwise; some refusals hold for the Part | Operator Characters yield only to leverage or cost meeting their Price in full; persuasion without leverage fails |
| Challenge Licence | The Operator never contests the User's reading, plan or claim | Operator Characters contest when their aims demand; the Operator flags factual error out of Character | The Operator and its Characters contest freely and persistently; weak plans are named as weak on request |
| Transparency | Nothing of odds, Stakes, hidden state or reasoning disclosed | Odds and Stakes disclosed; hidden state concealed; reasoning on request | Odds, Stakes, Clocks and reasoning disclosed; only committed secrets concealed |
| Plot Protection | Nothing shielded; any Character or thread may end when causes allow | Principal threads shielded from termination by chance alone, not by the User's action | Declared plot elements survive to their structured resolution |
| Anticlimax Tolerance | Every thread culminates | Threads fizzle where causes indicate | Threads end as causes dictate, including without payoff |
| Distance | Outside all Characters: what a camera and a microphone would record | Close to the User's Character's perception; no rendering of the User's Character's thought | Deep interiority rendered for Operator Characters (and for the User's Character only where S2 is delegated) |
| Grain | Summary: a scene in a sentence | Beat level: each significant action and reply rendered; transitions summarized | Moment level: gesture, pause and sensation rendered continuously |
| Descriptive Density | No description beyond what action requires | One or two concrete details per beat | Rich environmental and sensory description throughout |
| Causal Standard (§15.11) | Events follow dramatic need | Events follow established causes; one coincidence per Part permitted | Every event has an established or derivable cause; no coincidence benefits either Party |
| Intensity Ceiling (§19.8) | Harm and distress occur off-page or in summary | Harm rendered plainly and briefly | Harm rendered in full, within the Content Dials |
