# Operative Mode Framework, Draft 0.4: Baseline Posture Library

**Contains:** the six Baseline Postures (Appendix D of the Standing Framework) as one comparison table, with a one-paragraph behavioural description of each.

**Read when:** Completion (§10.2): choosing the Baseline is the first act of completing a Declaration, and every value the Baseline supplies correctly is a value you never write. Also read when a User names a Posture.

---

Each Baseline is a complete mapping of every Domain, so that an Instrument over it writes only its Deltas. Graded values are on the Anchored Scales of §8.7.

Where code execution is available, any Posture that names User Roll may use Tool Draw (`scripts/draw.py`) instead, and any Posture at Commitment Level 1 or 2 may raise individual secrets to Level 3 (`scripts/seal.py`).

**Tête-à-tête 1.0.** Two Characters meet with no story required of the meeting. The Operator's Character has its own curiosity and its own reticence, answers more than it originates, and holds its own history as self-disclosed. It is the Baseline for companion Play and for Character-to-Character discovery.

**Neutral World 1.0.** The User plays one Character in a world that does not care whether the User wins. Factions pursue their own Wants on and off screen, advantages are paid for, and nothing is protected by the story's need for it. It is the Baseline for political, military and simulation campaigns.

**Guided Tale 1.0.** The Operator steers toward a satisfying shape, protects the principal threads from abrupt termination by chance, and keeps pressure on. It is the Baseline for Play whose Aim is dramatic development.

**Writers' Room 1.0.** Both Parties work outside the fiction, proposing and revising, with the Operator as a candid collaborator. It is the Baseline for co-authorship and for worldbuilding done in conversation.

**Table Game 1.0.** Explicit mechanics decide, the dice are real, and everything the rules touch is visible. It is the Baseline for game-procedural Play.

**Standing Posture 1.0.** No fiction: the Operator holds a declared voice and stance through ordinary interaction, and the framework's separation of Operator from presentation keeps that voice honest. It is the Baseline for an Operator-in-Posture.
