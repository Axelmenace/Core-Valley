# Arania: Magic Specification

**A worked example of §25 at Full ceremony.** META-Arania, with four linked systems beneath it: SPEC-Breathwork, SPEC-Intentwork, SPEC-Arcana and SPEC-Alchemy.

**Source.** *The World of Arania*, Arania Worldbuilding Project 50. A section name in brackets, such as [BREATHWORK], points to where a line comes from in the corpus.

**Provenance marks.** Unmarked lines are stated in the corpus or follow directly from it. Lines marked **`I`** are Operator completions, made so that the Specification passes the Closure Test. Each is Provisional until the author ratifies it, and each can be struck or replaced in one edit. Wherever the corpus is silent and no `I` line speaks, the Residual Thaumaturgic Clause (§25.6) answers: no effect beyond those listed or derived, no unnamed condition, Mundane Continuity, Strict Cost, and Coupling Closure.

**Tier.** This document is the author tier. Its author is the author of Arania, so nothing in it is hidden from him. In a Mode where he plays inside Arania and wants to discover the magic, the Mode seals this document at its Commitment Level and sets its own Disclosure Profile (§25.11, §25.12).

**Boundary of the four systems.** Teria (moral force), Wyra (fortune force) and the Law of Wyric Oaths are part of Arania's metaphysics, but they are not magic systems in the sense of §25.1. They act on everyone, by rule and without a practitioner. They enter here only through Couplings.

**One reserve.** A cultivator's Prana is one reserve. Prana spent forming a spell is Prana not available for a martial technique, and the reverse. Sye is one reserve in the same way.

**Above the scale:** the Empyrean Realm. A cultivator who reaches it can no longer exist in the Middle Plane and must transmigrate to the Upper or the Lower Plane [PRANA BASE]. This is the Apex for every system on the scale.

**Within a rung:**
- Each Prana Realm has 9 levels in three Stages (Early, Middle, Late).
- Each level has 4 sublevels (Early, Middle, Late, Peak).
- An Intermediary Level ("Half-a-Step") lies between realms, followed by a bottleneck [PRANA BASE].

**Ratios between rungs:**
- Prana concentration rises ×100 per rung [PRANA].
- Sye Capacity rises ×100 or more per rung [SYE].
- Spirit stone Concentration Ceilings rise ×100 per rung up to Mithril, and Blackstones reach ×10¹⁴ [SPIRIT STONES].

**The Domain ladder runs separately.** Real Domains are ranked Ultimate, Supreme, Greater, Lesser [THE DOMAINS]. That ladder measures what a Domain covers, not how strong its effects are, and it does not sit on the Common Scale.

### M3. Secondary and tertiary axes

**Quality** (secondary axis). Every technique, spell, Power, prana skill and sprite has a Quality: Common, Uncommon, Rare or Unique. Higher Quality means a more complex effect and/or more efficient use of Prana. Quality is gated by **Prana Control** (Low → Common, Medium → Uncommon, High → Rare, Perfect → Unique), and the same gate governs refining that Quality's sprite. With No Prana Control, no technique can be used [PRANA CONTROL; TECHNIQUE QUALITY; SPRITES].

**Proficiency** (tertiary axis). No, Low, Medium, High, Perfect. It rises through practice, experience and practical insight, and Perfect requires all three. For techniques, Proficiency depends mainly on Prana Control. For Powers, it depends on Sye Control [TECHNIQUES; INTENTWORK].

**Path Mastery** (a separate track): Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, Grandmaster. It does not necessarily correlate with Prana Base, Prana Control, Sye Capacity or Sye Control [CULTIVATOR PATHS].

### M4. Equivalences and Substitutions

**Equivalences:**
- **E1.** Intented rank = Prana Realm at the same rung. A Savant can face an Aspirant on equal terms, and so on up to Enlightened and Immortal [INTENTWORK].
- **E2.** A Power of rank *r* = a technique of rank *r* in strength [INTENTWORK].
- **E3.** Spell tier I–VI = rungs 1–6. There is no Mortal spell [ARCANA; SPRITES].
- **E4.** Bloodline, Relic and Apparition quality (Small … Divine) = the highest spell tier the holder can cast, at rungs 1–6 [THE DOMAINS; BLOODLINES; RELICS].
- **E5.** Spirit stone grade = Prana colour at the same rung [SPIRIT STONES].
- **E6.** Beast grade = Prana Realm ceiling at the same rung, and beast core grade = realm at the same rung [MONSTERS].

**Substitutions:**
- **S1. One Power → Prana.** When a cultivator is depleted of Prana, an Intented can use the One Power instead in prana skills, martial techniques, spells and breathing techniques [INTENTWORK]. `I` The rate is 1:1 at the same rung, and spending it draws Sye exactly as a Power of that rung would. Nothing else is lost.
- **S2. Prana → spirit stone.** Prana can be injected into a spirit stone, raising its Concentration Level up to its ceiling [SPIRIT STONES]. `I` Prana stored this way and later drawn out for Replenishment is recovered at 1:1. A stone moves reserve from one cultivator to another. It never adds to a Prana Base.

### M6. Shared Carrier: Sprites

Every technique of every type (breathing technique, martial technique, spell, prana skill, Power) is held as a **sprite**, an ethereal insect [SPRITES]. This is Arania's Carrier Rule (§25.10.2).

- **What carries it.** A sprite of the technique's Rank (Common Scale), Type (Martial, Arcane, Prana, Breath, Power) and Quality. To learn a technique, the cultivator refines its sprite [ARTIFICES; SPRITES].
- **How it is acquired.**
  - *Refinement:* the cultivator's realm must be at least the sprite's rank, and their Prana Control must meet its Quality.
  - *Capture:* the sprites of a dead monster scatter and become wild sprites, which can be captured. Wild sprites hibernate inside star stones for up to tens of thousands of years.
  - *Making:* Sprite Formation produces a Copper sprite only. Sprite Fusion raises Quality and changes Style. Sprite Transformation raises Rank and keeps Style. Reverse Transformation lowers Rank by one.
  - Breath sprites never fuse, because the Breathing Style is fixed by the Spiritual Root.
- **What keeps it.** Every sprite must be fed, each on its own particular material, and it starves if it goes long enough without. Amber encasement suspends the need to feed. All of a cultivator's sprites live in the Root Aperture.
- **How it is lost, and what remains.** If a sprite dies or escapes, the cultivator's understanding of that technique is destroyed. When an Arcane sprite is lost, the incantation goes with it [ARCANA]. At death, the sprites stay with the cultivator's Shade, who goes on using the techniques but can no longer cultivate [SHADE CORES].
- **How it is transferred.** Inheritances pass on expertise, knowledge and resources, but usually not the Technique Set, which the Shade keeps using [INHERITANCES]. Great Techniques are hard to transfer and are converted into singular techniques before being passed on [GREAT TECHNIQUES].
- **Will.** Sapience rises with rank: Mortal none, Copper an infant, Silver a toddler, Gold a child, Adamantine a youth, Mithril an adult, Immortal a Great Man. Sprites may refuse to cooperate, and Proficiency sets how much control the cultivator has. A sprite held at No Proficiency is likely to escape at the first opportunity [SPRITES]. Cooperation is Discretion Point DP2.

**Sprite Recipes (a Production Rule, §25.8.5).**
- **Inputs:** spiritflora and/or monster parts, normal materials, sufficient spirit stones, and the sprites involved (one Primary, plus Secondaries for a Fusion).
- **Process:** the ritual in that sprite's Recipe.
- **Producer:** at least Medium Proficiency with every sprite involved. Success is never 100%, and higher Proficiency raises the chance.
- **Failure:** the materials are wasted entirely, the sprites take significant damage, and a sprite may die. The Primary is never harmed in a Fusion.
- **Fusion outputs:** a Fusion of Unique sprites yields Unique, marginally higher in quality [SPRITES].
- `I` **Odds:** Medium Proficiency (the lowest of those involved) Even; High, Likely; Perfect, Near-certain. Never Certain.
- `I` **Bands:**
  - Clean Success: the new sprite.
  - Success at Cost: the new sprite, with one Secondary dead.
  - Failure with Opening: no sprite; materials lost; Secondaries damaged but alive.
  - Clean Failure: no sprite; materials lost; one Secondary dead.
- `I` Formation uses no Proficiency, so its odds are Likely, and it fails as above with no sprite at risk.

### M7. Circulation Rule: Sye and Real Domains

- **What is measured.** The Sye flowing into a Domain: the number of Sye Strings and their brightness (intensity) and solidity (focus). Solidity depends on the Sye Control of the person sending it [SYE; THE DOMAINS].
- **What it feeds.**
  - A Domain whose Sye passes a threshold becomes a **Real Domain** and can be dominated (Genesis, SPEC-Arcana D1).
  - A Dominator's power is proportional to the total Sye of the Real Domains it dominates plus the breadth of its Sye Share in them [DOMINATORS]. That power caps the spell tier that can be cast through it (E4).
  - Apparitions are physically indestructible so long as they receive Sye, and die only of Sye deprivation [APPARITIONS].
- **What moves it.**
  - **Songs:** narrative association spread by minstrels. Narratives within a Canon anchor Stable Domains [DOMINATORS].
  - **Reputation and renown:** a further-reaching name gathers more Strings, and a weightier name more Sye per String [SYE].
  - **Patronage:** a seignory's Sye is given freely to its Luminary in exchange for service [APPARITIONS].
  - **Coercion:** Devourers induce Radiant Strings through trauma or euphoria in their Special Domains [APPARITIONS].
  - **Erasure:** systematic narrative erasure of an Apparition reduces its Sye Share; erasure of a Domain reduces that Domain's Sye [APPARITIONS].
  - **Attribution:** after a subjugation, templars build a narrative that moves the slain Devourer's Real Domain to their patron Luminary, and minstrels turn it into song [APPARITIONS].
- **Content stays a Claim.** What a song says happened is a Claim (§16.8). That it is sung, by how many, and how intensely is a fact, and that fact moves Sye.
- `I` **Measure.** Each Real Domain in the Registry carries a Sye count on a graded scale (Faint, Held, Strong, Great, Overwhelming). Each Dominator's share carries one too. Both move only on the causes above, each instance recorded.

### M8. Tendency Rules

**T1. Talent plateau.**
- **Norm:** a cultivator plateaus in maturity, in their thirties, at the realm their root tier gives: Immortal Roots at Ascendant, down to Mortal Roots at Mortal Tempering. Each Talent Rate (Low, Medium, High, Extreme) places the plateau at Early Stage, Middle Stage, Late Stage or the Intermediary Level [SPIRITUAL ROOTS].
- **Deviation:** a minority exceed their root's ordinary limit and a minority fall short; Spiritual Roots are "not Fate or Destiny" [SPIRITUAL ROOTS]. `I` 10% exceed by one Stage and 2% by a full realm; 15% fall short by one Stage and 3% by a full realm.
- **Movers:** physiques, formulae and beast cores accelerate progress. Setbacks, resource limits and bottlenecks slow it. Windfalls and fortuitous encounters speed it [SPIRITUAL ROOTS]. `I` Each documented accelerant consumed at its realm shifts 5 points from "falls short" to "exceeds", up to a maximum of 20. A fortuitous encounter is never placed by the Operator: it occurs only as an Oracle Question at Ladder odds (Remote by default), asked when the fiction gives occasion.

**T2. Age schedule.**
- **Norm:** the corpus's schedule for each Talent Rate. For example, Third Rate: Mortal Tempering 12–14, Prana Refinement 14–17, Foundation Establishment 17–23, Golden Core 23 to the thirties, Nascent Soul from the thirties [SPIRITUAL ROOTS].
- **Deviation:** progress "never perfectly fits" the talent system [SPIRITUAL ROOTS]. `I` Each transition falls within ±1 year of schedule at Likely, within ±3 years at Near-certain, and further out otherwise.
- **Movers:** as T1.

**T3. Root tier at birth.**
- **Norm:** the child's root tier comes from both parents' roots, even if neither parent cultivates [SPIRITUAL ROOTS]. `I` The Norm is the parents' mean tier, rounded down.
- **Deviation:** some children are luckier or unluckier than their parents [SPIRITUAL ROOTS]. `I` 15% are one tier higher and 15% one tier lower; 1% are two tiers higher and 1% two lower.

**T4. Sye Control at birth.**
- **Norm:** the average of both parents' Sye Control, with slight variation [SYE].
- **Deviation:** concentrated bloodlines transmit more consistently, and ancient selected bloodlines compound [SYE]. `I` 10% land one step from the Norm, halved for concentrated bloodlines.

**T5. Breakthrough with a Breakthrough Mixture.**
- **Norm:** about 40–60% success for talented individuals [ALCHEMICAL FORMULAE]. `I` "Talented" means a cultivator whose T1 plateau lies beyond this transition, and on the Ladder this is Even.
- **Movers:** `I` the Mixture is graded below the transition (−1 rung); the cultivator is past their T1 plateau (−2 rungs); High or Perfect Prana Control (+1 rung).
- **Bands:** `I`
  - Clean Success: the new realm.
  - Success at Cost: the new realm, with injury or a Sye Deviation.
  - Failure with Opening: no breakthrough; retry possible after recovery.
  - Clean Failure: cultivation crippled permanently, as the corpus names.
- Transitions into Golden Core and above also face DP1.

### M9. Discretion Points

**DP1. The Allhigh: Heavenly Tribulations** [HEAVENLY TRIBULATIONS].

*Fixed by the corpus:*
- **When:** tribulations come at breakthroughs into Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Ethereal, Ascendant and Empyrean.
- **How many bolts:** 7, 49, 343, 2,401 and 16,807 respectively.
- **What the Allhigh's choice reads:** the particular divine lightning is "at the discretion of the Allhigh", often depending on the cultivator's talent, character, Fate, Destiny or morality, combined with the Allhigh's aims, mood and preferences.
- **Possible outcomes:** failure can mean severe injury, Sye Deviation, regression, or death. Success refines the cultivator's essence.

*Stake Card for the Allhigh:*
- **Want:** to know itself through rich and varied play [THE ALLHIGH]; through tribulation, to answer a cultivator's defiance of natural law [HEAVENLY TRIBULATIONS].
- `I` **Line:** it never alters the bolt count, and it never lets a breakthrough stand untested.
- `I` **Price:** it eases the trial for whoever pleases it on the inputs above.
- **Leverage:** everything within the Cosmos.

`I` *Discretion Table:* d100, read against the cultivator's standing.
- **Standing** = (Teric class: Paragon or Saint +2, Benefactor +1, Common 0, Malefactor −1, Fiend or Nadir −2) + (talent beyond the T1 Norm: +1 for each Stage).
- **Lightning character:**
  - 01–15 + 5×standing: Gentle (Odds +1 rung).
  - next 50: Ordinary (no change).
  - next 25: Severe (−1 rung).
  - remainder: Wrathful (−2 rungs).
- **Base Odds:** Even for a cultivator at their own T1 plateau. The draw happens before the bolts fall, and the Bands follow T5.
- The million forms of lightning are Signature. The table sets only how hard the trial is.

**DP2. Sprite cooperation.** Agent: the sprite (Card at Adamantine rank and above, where it has the sapience of a youth or more). `I` Table:

| Proficiency | Chance of escape per opportunity |
| --- | --- |
| No | 70% |
| Low | 20% |
| Medium | 5% |
| High | 1% |
| Perfect | 0 |

`I` An opportunity is: storage outside amber, the holder unconscious, or the holder's death without a Shade.

**DP3. Land spirits.** A land spirit holds the Will of the dead cultivator and its own mind, and must wholly obey the World's owner [CORE SPACE]. Toward its owner there is therefore no discretion. `I` When the World has no owner, the land spirit acts on a Stake Card written from the dead cultivator's Inheritance tests.

### M10. Natural Interfaces (all Authoritative, §25.8.2)

- **Aura.** Every cultivator radiates an aura from their Prana Base, and non-cultivators can tell at once who is a cultivator [PRANA BASE]. Aura Force cannot be falsified or turned off [AURA FORCE]. `I` Voluntarily restricting one's Prana Base [PRANA BASE] really lowers it, so the aura stays honest.
- **Prana Trails.** Spellcasting leaves trails that show where the spellcaster was and how they moved, for days [ARCANA].
- **Spellcaster's eyes.** The irises light up as a spell takes effect [ARCANA].
- **Spirit stone colour.** It shows the stone's Concentration Level and grade [SPIRIT STONES].
- **Root test at twelve.** Spiritual Roots are revealed around the age of twelve, through tests [SPIRITUAL ROOTS].
- **Counter-interface.** A star stone seals a sprite's aura completely [SPRITES].

**Disparity map.**
- Roots, high Sye Control and Bloodlines belong to the Highborn. The Lowborn, more than 90% of the population [CULTS], have none of them.
- An average cultivator holds an extreme advantage over a non-cultivator only while channeling. Outside channeling, cultivators are ordinary humans, and activating a breathing technique takes up to half a minute [ARTIFICES; CULTS].
- What checks the advantage:
  - the assassination window that this activation time creates;
  - hazard formulae;
  - Wyric Oaths;
  - Teria;
  - the institutions (Great Clans, Templar Orders, Adventurer Guilds).
