# Magic Specification (§25)

Hand this back filled, never blank. Fill one Specification for each Substrate (§25.4.1). Every line is author-tier and hard. Softness belongs to the Lens Sheets and the Disclosure Profile, never to this sheet.

Most systems are broad: fill Part D as an Effect Grammar (§25.8). Use the Catalogue form only for a narrow system. At Light ceremony, a Grammar system uses Parts A to C, one line per C in Part E, and the Core Grammar; a narrow system uses at most six one-line effects (§25.16). The Residual Thaumaturgic Clause (§25.6) answers everything the sheet leaves unsaid. Record any line of the Clause you displace. Where the setting has several systems, fill the Metaphysic sheet first, then one Specification per system beneath it.

## Worked example 1: a broad system (Light ceremony)

### SPEC-Lattice · v1.0.0 · Level 3 · sha256:‹digest›

**A.**
- **Seed:** the world runs on a lattice of mana that answers anyone who has learned its patterns, and pays out by rank.
- **Boundary:** all spellcraft and skills. Divine miracles are SPEC-Grace, linked by Interface.
- **Baseline:** Earth physics.

**B.**
- **Substrate:** ambient mana lattice.
- **Operation:** the caster shapes a pattern, using a known spell or one improvised from a Domain they hold → the lattice draws mana from the caster's pool → the effect manifests at the pattern's Tier.
- **Invariants:** effects conserve energy beyond the mana spent; nothing is created from nothing above Tier 5.
- **Prohibitions:** no resurrection; no reading of memory; no time travel.
- **Generativity:** Grammar.

**C.**
- Transference 1: affinity is born, and Domains are learned.
- Prevalence 3.
- Source: internal, renewable; full after a night's rest; Depletion Effect: unconsciousness at zero.
- Flux 0 for the world; positive per person under Progression.
- Naturalness 3.
- Ease of Use 2.
- Reliability 3: improvised spells start one rung lower than codexed ones.
- Consistency 3: affinity limits which Domains a caster can hold.

**D1.**
- **Domains:** Flame · Frost · Earth · Wind · Body · Mind (surface only: no memory) · Space (short range only).
- **Tiers 1 to 7.** Area (radius) and Range double per Tier, from 2 m and 10 m at T1, so T5 reaches 32 m and 160 m. Magnitude: T1 a lit match → T4 a house fire → T7 a city block. Duration: T1 one minute, T3 ten minutes, T5 one hour, T7 one day. Gate Ceiling: T1–2 at 1, T3–5 at 2, T6–7 at 3 (admissible only where the Instrument admits it). Default rung: Likely at the caster's own Tier, one rung down per Tier above it.
- **Cost:** mana = 2^Tier. Each Modifier adds 50%, rounded up.
- **Modifiers:** silent casting; ×2 range; ×2 duration; +1 target. Two or more Modifiers on one effect raise its Tier by one.
- **Access:** a caster's Rank sets their highest Tier. Rank rises on the caster's tenth Clean Success at their own Tier since their last rise, counted in the Record.
- **Combination:** two active effects at most; like effects do not stack; a multi-Domain effect is priced in each Domain and pays the higher Price.
- **Apex:** Tier 7. Nothing alters a Prohibition or affects more than one city block.
- **Interface:** a status window, Authoritative. It shows the caster's own Rank, pool and Codexed spells, and nothing about anyone else.
- **Benchmarks:** T1 *Spark* (light a fire within 1 m); T3 *Firebolt* (destroy a door at 40 m); T5 *Quake* (collapse a hall); T7 *Firestorm* (raze a city block).
- **Signature Effect:** the User's Character's *Appraisal*. It reads the stat line of anything touched. It is retained as a Disparity, bounded by touch range and three uses a day.

**E.**
- **Costs:** mana.
- **Consequences:** a caster below a quarter of their pool takes a rung down on all draws.
- **Countermeasures:** opposed casting at an equal or higher Tier; null-iron.

**Pricing one improvised request.** "Freeze the 30 m-wide ford hard enough for the army to cross over two hours." Domain: Frost. Placement: an Area of 30 m falls at T5 (32 m), and a Duration of two hours needs one ×2 duration Modifier on T5's one hour. One Modifier does not raise the Tier. The Parity check against *Quake* holds. Price: 32 × 1.5 = 48 mana. A Rank 4 caster cannot reach it, so a Rank 5 caster is needed. Entered in the Codex as *Ford-Ice*, T5, 48.

**Disclosure.** Target Hardness 3, earned. The status window is the main disclosure channel, so a Rank-up shows the Tier's Envelope.

## Worked example 2: a narrow system (Light ceremony)

### SPEC-Brine · v1.0.0 · Level 3 · sha256:‹digest›

**A.**
- **Seed:** a word written in salt is true until the salt dissolves.
- **Boundary:** saltwriting only. The tide-cults' rites are SPEC-Tide, linked by Interface.
- **Baseline:** Earth physics.

**B.**
- **Substrate:** salt evaporated from seawater holds an imperative that is written into it.
- **Operation:** the writer traces a word of the Old Littoral in brine-salt on a surface → the surface obeys the word → the effect ends when the salt dissolves or is broken.
- **Invariants:** a script binds only what it is written on. One word per script. Strength scales with the mass of salt.
- **Prohibitions:** salt cannot bind living flesh; nothing written can bring something back.
- **Interfaces:** water dissolves it by Mundane Continuity; SPEC-Tide's rites can dissolve salt at range.
- **Generativity:** Rule. Any Littoral imperative the writer knows produces its literal effect on the surface it is written on.

**C.**
- Transference 3: anyone taught the letters can write.
- Prevalence 2: coastal guilds only.
- Source: external, finite per script; Depletion Effect: the script fails silently.
- Flux 0.
- Naturalness 2.
- Ease of Use 2: two years to learn the letters; seconds to write.
- Reliability 3: a misformed letter fails on Remote; interrupted by water and wind.
- Consistency 4.

**D.**
- E1, *Hold*: a door or lid will not open. Range touch; Duration until the salt dissolves; Mag ~ a grown man's strength per handful; Success Likely; Gate Ceiling 1.
- E2, *Bear*: a beam or plank takes ten times its load. Same Parameters as E1.
- E3, *Hush*: a surface makes no sound. Gate Ceiling 0.

**E.**
- **Cost:** the salt itself; Brine-salt is scarce inland.
- **Consequence:** the hands crack and bleed after a day's writing.
- **Countermeasures:** water, rain, scouring.

**Lens: the User's Character (dockside apprentice).** Hardness 2. Known: E1, E3, and dissolution by water, all from the master's teaching. Believed: "a spoken word strengthens the script" ✗.

**Disclosure.** Target Hardness 2 → 3, earned. Odds shown.
