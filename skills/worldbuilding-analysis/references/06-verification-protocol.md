# 06 The Verification Protocol: Coherence as Hypothesis

**Contains:** Foundational Commitments F1 to F4 (defeasibility and Axiom Force, consistency as hypothesis, Lore as proof calculus, Stance discipline); the Conflict Typology (L, S, P with severities, Q); Steps V1 to V7; the patch cost order; the Certification Ladder C0 to C4; the Reconciliation Ledger; Cross-Substrate Yield; the Verification Principles.

**Read when:** coherence must be tested rather than assumed; a conflict needs typing or reconciling; a witness sweep is planned; a certificate is issued, cited or reopened.

---

The protocol operationalizes the framework's central promise: that a System can be *known* to be coherent, and that there is a point at which the knowing is done. It corrects three defects that appear when licensure is handled as classical entailment: the wrong logic (derivation is defeasible, not monotonic), a question-begging premise (axiom consistency assumed rather than tested), and a missing finish line ("consistent so far" is not consistent, and an unexercised System cannot be certified at all).

It extends the Coherence Audit (Analysis Step 5) from local checks to joint verification of the System as a whole. Its working object is the Conceit Graph: every patch, witness and revision adds Nodes and links, and verification is the discipline of re-scanning it.

## Foundational Commitments

**F1: Derivation is defeasible; Licensure is total; Force is declared.** A System over its *complete* Substrate licenses its Lore totally and timelessly. No derivation ever runs over the complete Substrate, so every derivation is defeasible: it *follows plausibly*, it is not *proven*. Adding Substrate can defeat a previously licensed conclusion without retracting any axiom: a defeated license is a derivation that mis-specified the Substrate, not a defeated System. Three consequences:

- Two elements can each be validly derived from a consistent System and still conflict. Colliding defaults are normal in non-monotonic derivation. That settles nothing about a given case; the protocol's job is to discriminate missing scope, missing priority, and genuine inconsistency.
- No conflict is permanent evidence of inconsistency, and no reconciliation is permanent proof of consistency.
- Every derivation is indexed to the Substrate current when it ran. When the Substrate grows, earlier derivations are re-checkable, not sacred.

Revision 05 adds what makes typing possible. Every System carries a declared **Axiom Force**, the distinction defeasible logic draws between strict rules, defeasible rules and defeaters:

- **Strict:** exceptionless. Prohibitions, invariants, conservation laws. Derivations through Strict Systems alone are strict.
- **Default:** holds absent a defeater. Most institutional and cultural Systems.
- **Tendency:** distributional, with Norm, Deviation and Movers.

A Type L conflict is possible only between strict branches. Without Force, L, S and P cannot be told apart, and every typing is a guess.

**F2: Consistency is the hypothesis, never the premise.** The consistency of a rich informal axiom set cannot be inspected by reading the axioms. It is a conjecture that gains support only through survived derivation. The total licensure structure (System over complete Substrate) is never inspectable; it is probed only by defeasible derivations from partial context, which is why a certified level is the strongest claim the protocol can issue. The protocol is the epistemology of an inaccessible ontology. No audit may open from "the axioms are consistent, so the conflict must be contextual": the conflict under examination is often the only evidence on the question. The System's status is cited as a certification level (V7), never asserted flatly.

**F3: Lore is the proof calculus.** Under the Structuralist Position, Lore is never the goal, but derivation is the only instrument that exhibits coherence: derivations are generated expressions that test licensure. The tier therefore splits by role:

- **Committed Lore:** content the author adopts. It earns its place by illuminating or exercising structure.
- **Witness Lore:** derivations run solely to exercise the System. A witness is scaffolding. Its result is logged in the Ledger; the material may be kept, promoted to committed status, or discarded without loss.

Unpopulated remains a finished state. Unexercised is the verification deficit. This turns "System-strong, Lore-starved" from an aesthetic remark into a measured deficit, without touching intentional sparsity.

**F4: Only Authorial statements conflict.** Lens content never enters the conflict queue (`01-ontology.md` §1.5). An in-world chronicle that contradicts the System is in-world error. What *can* conflict is the Authorial record of the belief, when no mechanism of error explains why it is held: that is a Type P strain on the Culture or Mind System, and it enters the queue as such.

## The Conflict Typology

The unqualified word "contradiction" is retired. Every apparent conflict is typed at intake, because the four types demand different treatments.

**Type L: Logical conflict.** P and not-P strictly derivable: both branches run through Strict Systems only. Rare in natural-language Systems; most claimed L conflicts are misclassified S or P, and checking the Force of each System on both branches usually shows it. A genuine strict derivation is maximally informative: it proves either a hidden premise (make it explicit, and the conflict usually reclassifies) or genuine inconsistency of the axiom set (V5).

**Type S: Scope collision.** Systems conflict only when co-applied to a Domain at least one was never written to govern. Cure: explicit scope or priority conditions. The cheapest reconciliation class, but scope-narrowing must still pass the Retraction Test, since shrinking a System's Domain can be a retraction in disguise.

**Type P: Plausibility strain.** Jointly derived elements that strain the derivation without formal contradiction: a ruling class instituting its own demonization; a granted affordance nobody uses. Graded and theory-laden, resolved by plausibility arguments, not consistency proofs. Cure: causal-historical context that raises joint plausibility (the Sar Elay maneuver). Severity is recorded at intake:

- **P1:** a reader's charitable default resolves it; no context need be stated.
- **P2:** resolution needs stated context, which does not yet exist in the record.
- **P3:** absent new context, the joint derivation is less plausible than its negation.

A P1 or P2 strain may be accepted as it stands, with its rationale written. That option does not exist for L, S or Q.

**Type Q: Magnitude conflict.** Stated quantities violate a budget the System implies: carrying capacity, demography, throughput, energy, time (`04-structural-profile.md` §4.7.4). Q conflicts are computed, not argued, and they are usually closed by adjusting a parameter, which is cheap but still faces the Retraction Test (does the number the setting was built around survive?). A Q conflict that no parameter closes reclassifies as L or P.

## The Steps

### V1: Intake and Typing

Catalogue every apparent conflict, whether found by the Coherence Audit (including hard Constraint violations, Optimizer and Magnitude checks), met during generative work, or minted by a witness sweep (V6). For each: assign the type (with severity for P), record the full derivation path of both branches as Substrate-indexed license chains and Generation Edges (which Systems, of what Force, over what Substrate, through which links), and open a Ledger entry.

### V2: Bounded Reconciliation

Attempt reconciliation strictly by added System-context. The attempt is bounded: by default, three candidate patches per conflict per round. On failure the entry is marked **open**, neither resolved nor condemned: a failed search is evidence about the searcher, never a verdict on the System.

**The patch cost order.** Candidate patches are tried, and preferred, cheapest first:

| Cost | Patch class | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Explication** | Nothing. The premise was already in the record; the conflict was a misreading |
| 1 | **Scope or priority condition** | A refinement of existing Systems: where each governs, which yields |
| 2 | **Substrate or context** | Causal-historical context, an Election, a Boundary fact; no new System |
| 2 | **Parameter adjustment** | A changed quantity (Type Q's usual cure) |
| 3 | **New System** | A new primitive, which also creates a coverage obligation in the next sweep |

Among patches that pass both gates (V3), the cheapest wins; at equal cost, the greater fecundity yield.

### V3: Patch Validity Gates

A candidate patch that closes the conflict passes two gates before the closure counts.

**Gate 1, the Retraction Test.** Enumerate the consequences the affected System was introduced to license. The patch passes iff those consequences survive outside the conflict zone. A patch that strips a System of its original extension is a revision wearing context's costume: reclassify it as surgery (V5) and book it so.

**Gate 2, the Fecundity Criterion.** The patch independently licenses at least one new Lore pattern that (i) was not needed to close the conflict and (ii) is plausible on its own merits. Enrichment proves itself by excess content, not by mere closure. An **Explication** passes Gate 2 trivially: it adds no context, so there is no excess to demand.

Outcomes. Both gates passed: **fecund reconciliation**; the new System-context is banked and the entry closes. Gate 1 passed, Gate 2 failed: **ad hoc patch**; admitted provisionally, entered in the Debt Register, scheduled for replacement or promotion. A debt patch that later shows fecundity is promoted. One that accumulates dependents without ever showing fecundity is a degeneration signal (V5).

### V4: Global Co-application

Once per round, over all patches minted in that round, never per patch: (1) collect the round's patches; (2) co-apply them pairwise on every shared Domain; (3) re-run the conflict scan over the enlarged System. **A conflict introduced by a patch is charged to the patch**, not logged as a fresh discovery. Keep net-conflict accounting per round: a round that closes fewer conflicts than its patches open is degenerating, however it feels locally. Local closure under jointly incompatible patches is the failure this step exists to catch.

### V5: Escalation to Axiom Surgery

A conflict is ruled genuine (the System, not the searcher, is the problem) when any of these holds:

- **Overdetermination:** it re-derives through two or more independent paths.
- **Degeneration:** successive rounds show negative net-conflict accounting, or the Debt Register grows without promotions.
- **Retraction exhaustion:** every patch that closes it fails the Retraction Test.

Treatment: minimal revision of the System; re-run every derivation the revised System took part in (the checker's `strike` lists them); re-check every Ledger entry whose path touched it. Log the revision as the highest-value finding the protocol produces. A forced revision is the System correcting its own foundations, worth more than any quantity of successful enrichment.

### V6: The Witness Sweep

Verification is sought, not stumbled into. Derive Lore-tier consequences systematically, to declared coverage targets:

- **System coverage:** every committed Authorial System exercised by at least one witness. Order the sweep by critical load, highest first.
- **Interaction coverage:** System pairs co-exercised on shared Domains. The surface is combinatorial, so declare the depth and prioritize in two classes: **co-resident pairs first** (constitutive of Domainhood, per the Domain Coherence Criterion), then **cross-Domain pairs ordered by Channel capacity**.
- **Generation coverage:** every Persistent Relation examined for Emergence (its Edge derived, or its absence justified), and every Generation Edge's covering System exercised by a witness that names the Edge.

**Adversarial witness classes.** Standard witnesses derive the expected. These are designed to break things, and the higher certification levels require them:

| Class | Derives |
|---|---|
| **Boundary** | Cases at a System's scope edge, or at a Domain boundary where two Systems meet |
| **Extremal** | Parameters at their extremes: the largest army, the longest drought, the strongest practitioner |
| **Optimizer** | What a competent in-world agent does with the System's affordances (`04-structural-profile.md` §4.7.3) |
| **Long-run** | The setting's loops run forward over the span it claims: does the stated stability hold? |
| **Magnitude** | The budgets of §4.7.4, computed |

Tag witness material `[LORE·witness]` with its genesis note. Every witness logs its path and outcome: clean, or conflict (intake at V1). Witnesses are discardable after logging: the proof matters; the theorem's witness need not be kept.

### V7: The Completion Certificate

A System is certified at a level of the **Certification Ladder** when every requirement of that level and all levels below it holds. The checker computes the level from the graph and names the next level's blockers.

| Level | Name | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **C0** | Mapped | The Conceit Graph is recorded and has no framework violations |
| **C1** | Exercised | Every committed System exercised; conflict queue empty (every entry closed, or Type P accepted with rationale); Debt Register empty or every item accepted with rationale; the latest full sweep minted zero new conflicts |
| **C2** | Co-resident | Every co-resident System pair co-exercised |
| **C3** | Adjacent | Every System pair across high-capacity persistent Channels co-exercised; every Generation Edge exercised; every Persistent Relation examined; at least one resolved Optimizer witness and one resolved Long-run witness |
| **C4** | Saturated | Every System pair across every persistent Channel co-exercised; a resolved Magnitude witness; two consecutive fixed-point sweeps |

Custom parameters may be added to any level (a witness chain length, a neighborhood depth) and are stated on the certificate. A certificate states its level, its parameters, the date, and the graph's seal (`seal`), so it certifies one exact graph. It is explicitly fallibilist: it is not a consistency proof, since no such proof exists for a rich informal System, and any later derivation that mints a conflict reopens it. This is the finish line the bare phrase "internally consistent" fails to define: not consistency *simpliciter*, which is unattainable, but *exercised to level C without unresolved conflict*, which is attainable, honest and citable. ## The Reconciliation Ledger

One entry per conflict, maintained across rounds and settings. Template: `assets/reconciliation-ledger.md`.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID / Setting | Unique reference and home setting |
| Type | L / S / P (with severity) / Q |
| Branch Force | The Force of the Systems on each branch |
| Derivation paths | Substrate-indexed license chains and Generation Edges of both branches |
| Attempts | Candidate patches tried, each with its cost class, against budget |
| Outcome | Fecund reconciliation / explication / debt patch / promotion / surgery / accepted (P1 or P2 only) / open |
| Context minted | The new System-context, as `[SYS]` with an id |
| Fecundity yield | New Lore independently licensed by the patch |
| Charged conflicts | Conflicts this entry's patch introduced (V4) |
| Status date | Last action |

The Ledger is the audit trail V4 and V5 require. It turns the generativity thesis into a measured quantity (fecund reconciliations over total conflicts is a ratio the corpus reports, not an article of faith). And it is the substrate for the extension below.

## Extension: Cross-Substrate Yield

Where one System engine runs in several settings, log where identical Systems license **divergent** Lore under different Substrates, and where they license identical Lore regardless. Divergence points are experimental yield: the Substrate doing demonstrable causal work. Identity across Substrates means the Substrate is decorative at that System. The step is optional per setting, but it is the only operation that turns a multi-setting corpus from one theorem restated in different notation into the repeated experiment it structurally resembles. Run generatively, the same operation is Transformational Variation (`07-generative-mode.md`).

## Verification Principles

- Never assert or assume a System's consistency; cite its certification level.
- The unqualified word "contradiction" is a category error; every conflict is typed, and its type follows from the Force of its branches.
- A failed reconciliation is an open conflict, never a verdict of inconsistency.
- Prefer the cheapest patch that passes both gates.
- Closure without fecundity is debt, not progress.
- Conflicts caused by a patch are charged to the patch.
- Lens content does not conflict; unexplained belief does.
- Witness Lore is instrumental and freely discardable. Unexercised System is the only content-level defect this protocol names.
- Certificates are dated, sealed, levelled and fallibilist. A certificate reopens the moment a derivation conflicts, and the reopening is a finding, not an embarrassment.
