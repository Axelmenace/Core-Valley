# 10 Revision 05 Notes: the Audit of Revision 04

**Contains:** the method of the audit; Part A, the internal conflicts of Revision 04 typed and reconciled under its own Verification Protocol; Part B, the gaps in the framework itself; Part C, the defects of the skill as packaged; Part D, what was kept; Part E, the migration map and how to migrate an existing analysis.

**Read when:** the user asks what changed from Revision 04 and why; an analysis made under Revision 04 is being carried forward; a term from Revision 04 needs its successor.

---

## Method

Revision 04 of this skill (archived in the Core-Valley repository as `archive/worldbuilding-framework-revision-04.md`) was audited the way it audits settings. A framework that certifies coherence should survive its own Verification Protocol, so each internal conflict was typed (L, S or P), reconciled by the cheapest patch, and the patch held to the Retraction Test (Gate 1: the original consequences survive) and the Fecundity Criterion (Gate 2: the patch licenses something new). Things the framework needs and lacks are not conflicts; they are closure failures of the framework, and are listed separately in Part B, as a Structuralist separates undecided structure from inconsistent structure. Part C covers the skill's engineering, which is a different kind of defect again.

No Revision 04 conflict proved to be Type L. That is itself a finding about Revision 04's quality: every collision was a scope written too broadly or a priority left implicit, which is the cheap kind.

| Core Rules 1 to 4 | Immanence and the Finding Discipline |

**Migrating an existing Revision 04 analysis:**

1. Relabel every Entailment link as a Determined license; demote to Elected each one whose target was an authorial choice.
2. Give every `[SYS]` statement a Force.
3. Decompose every Conceit that the consequential dimension moved to System (foundings, coups, singular agents) into its rule and its genesis.
4. Reassign anything filed under the Institutions or Individuals Fields: institutions to their Culture, Economy or Polity Domain; individuals to Positions.
5. Mark the Stance of every in-world document and belief.
6. Map each Revision 04 certificate to the highest ladder level whose requirements its recorded coverage meets, or re-run the sweep. Re-seal.
