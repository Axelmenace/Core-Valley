# 01 Ontology: Tiers, Licensure, Substrate, Stance, Fields and Domains

**Contains:** the definition of Worldbuilding; the three Tiers and the Modal Test; Decomposition and the Dominance Rule; Licensure (Determined and Elected); the Substrate; Stance (Authorial and Lens); Conceits, systems and Positions; the Field profile; Domains, Realized States, the Domain and System Coherence Criteria; Adjacency, Contact and Channels; the Asymmetry Gradient; Configuration.

**Read when:** assigning a tier, deciding whether something is System or Lore, handling in-world documents or beliefs, recording Domains and Channels, or explaining any of these terms.

---

## 1.1 Worldbuilding

**Worldbuilding** is the construction of systems, the deduction of the patterns those systems license, the election of instances within the licensed space, and the controlled transmission of all three to an audience with limited cognitive bandwidth.

The definition names four operations and only one of them is construction. The other three are deduction, election and transmission. A Structuralist work may consist of the first operation alone, carried to closure (see `04-structural-profile.md`, Closure), and be finished.

## 1.2 The three Tiers

**I. Systembuilding: "Systems over time."** Nomic statements: laws, prohibitions, tendencies, invariants, decision functions. Properties: Structural, Abstract, Causal, and **substrate-general**. Statement form: *"X happens"*, *"Y behave like this"*, *"Z produces these effects over time"*, *"no X can Y"*.

**II. Lorebuilding: "Patterns within time."** Historical regularities of the realized run: institutions, dynasties, movements, cycles, conspiracies, large events. What historians of the setting would write. Properties: Institutional, Medium-term, and **substrate-specific**.

**III. Narrativebuilding: "Instances in time."** Singular occurrences at agent-experiential grain: events, characters, individual histories. Properties: Particular, Concrete, Experiential. In narrative-borne media it is the only tier the audience contacts directly; in codex-borne media it is not (see `08-transmission.md`).

### 1.2.1 The Modal Test

A statement's tier is fixed by its modal force. The test varies the substrate and asks what survives:

1. **Would the statement hold over every substrate within its declared scope?** Then it is **System**. Its signature is counterfactual support: "were another march founded on this coast, its crews too would form oath-fellowships."
2. **Does it describe a recurring, durable or aggregate pattern of this setting's realized run?** Then it is **Lore**. It supports no counterfactual on its own; the mechanism that would support one is a System, and pairing each Lore regularity with the System that licenses it is the ordinary work of Tier Mapping.
3. **Does it name a single occurrence at agent-experiential grain?** Then it is **Narrative**.

Three statements about one phenomenon:

- "Any dynasty under partible inheritance fragments within three generations." System.
- "The Varn dynasties have each fragmented within three generations." Lore.
- "Ottar divided the march among his four sons in the spring of 612." Narrative.

Scope does not decide tier. "The Throne of Varn passes to the eldest daughter" governs a single institution, but it quantifies over every possible succession within it: a System of narrow scope. Breadth is a property of a System's Domain, not of its tier.

### 1.2.2 Decompose, then assign: the Dominance Rule

Many Conceits bundle statements of several tiers. A founding is a singular event that institutes a rule. An institutional superintelligence is a particular agent whose decision function is law over its Domain. Such a Conceit is handled in two moves.

1. **Decompose** the bundle into its nomic, historical and singular components. Link them: the instituted rule's genesis is the founding; the agent's history is licensed by its decision function.
2. **The Dominance Rule.** Wherever a process or instance registers legibly across several scales along different dimensions (temporal, spatial, aggregative, consequential), the dimension on which it reaches System-tier scale takes priority, and analysis resides there. Tier assignment is a maximum across dimensions, not an average. The lower components are available only as genesis notes or as licensed illustration (a zoom-in for demonstration), never as the resident grain of analysis.

So a coup whose output is standing structure is analyzed at System grain, as the rule it installed, with the coup recorded as that rule's genesis. A singular agent with system-scale duration and breadth is Systems material, as its policy. What the Modal Test adds is protection for the middle tier: the dynasty, the movement and the institution's history remain Lore, because they are patterns of the realized run. The Dominance Rule claims only their nomic content.

**Tiers are scales of one phenomenon** (Braudel's *longue durée*, *conjoncture* and *histoire événementielle*). Dynamic analysis (the system in motion, fault lines propagating, architecture evolving under stress) is Systemsbuilding: temporal extension does not change grain. What tips analysis into Narrative is descent to agent-experiential resolution.

## 1.3 Licensure

The tier relation is **Licensure**. A System, run over a Substrate (1.4), licenses a space of possible runs: the **Licensed Space**. A Lore pattern is licensed when it lies in that space. A Narrative instance is licensed by Lore, and through Lore by System.

The relation is the one structural linguistics draws between *langue* and *parole*: the System is the structure that admits an unbounded set of realizations, and the setting's recorded history is one realization at two grains. This is the ground of the Structuralist claim that an unpopulated System can be finished: a structure is complete when its Licensed Space is decided, whether or not any of that space is realized.

### 1.3.1 Determined and Elected

Every license carries a strength.

- **Determined.** The statement holds in every licensed run over the recorded substrate. This is **Entailment** proper, the special case Revision 04 took for the whole relation.
- **Elected.** The statement holds in some licensed runs, and obtains because the author chose it, or because a declared draw selected it. An Election is recorded, and from then on it joins the Substrate: later derivations run over it. This is how path dependence enters the record.

Two further conditions are failures of licensure:

- **Unlicensed.** The statement holds in no licensed run. It is a Constraint violation, and intake for the Verification Protocol (V1).
- **Ungrounded.** The statement has no recorded license at all. Either its System is implicit and must be surfaced, or it is a grounding gap.

The **Determination Ratio** (Determined Lore over Determined plus Elected Lore, committed Authorial Lore only) measures how much of the realized setting the System forces. It is reported, never maximized as policy: a setting may keep its history deliberately contingent. It answers a Structuralist question directly: where is the author's hand, and where is the structure's?

### 1.3.2 Licensing and Inference

Licensure has two readings, as Entailment did in Revision 04.

- **Licensing (top-down):** deriving expressions along the license. System licenses Lore; Lore licenses Narrative. Licensing is the Structuralist's operation.
- **Inference (bottom-up):** abducing the licensing structure from its instances. Narrative infers Lore; Lore infers System. Inference is the Contentialist's operation, and the only operation available to an audience that contacts nothing but instances.

**The Expression Rule.** Both readings always exist. A licensed Conceit always follows plausibly from its licensing structure, and that structure is always in principle abducible from its instances. Whether an audience *registers* an Inferential reading is a separate question (`08-transmission.md`).

## 1.4 The Substrate

The **Substrate** is everything a System runs over that is not itself System. It has four parts.

- **Base:** the base setting, a recognizable historical period or genre template. It supplies the ambient defaults, and in transmission it is the compression algorithm (`08-transmission.md`).
- **Initial State:** the setting's starting configuration: geography, populations, endowments, the arrangement of Domains at the opening.
- **Boundary:** exogenous inputs over time that no recorded System governs: a comet, an invasion from off the map, an intervention from outside the setting's laws.
- **Elections:** authorial choices among licensed runs, appended in order.

Substrate declarations are **Stipulated** roots of the Conceit Graph (role `substrate`). They carry a tier by grain, and most are Lore. Every derivation is indexed to the Substrate current when it ran (F1, `06-verification-protocol.md`).

**The Recurrent Boundary test.** A Boundary input that recurs on a schedule is a missing System. An exogenous shock that arrives regularly is a law of something, and the analysis should find what.

## 1.5 Stance

Every statement has a **Stance**.

- **Authorial:** asserted by the setting, and true in it.
- **Lens:** held by a population, institution or agent inside the setting: belief, doctrine, myth, propaganda, folk theory, in-world scholarship. Notation `@Lens(holder)`; in the graph, `"stance": "lens:<holder>"`.

The Stance rules:

1. **The holding is Authorial; the content is Lens.** That the Tide Cult teaches the tide is a god's breath is an Authorial Conceit, usually Lore in the Culture Field. That the tide *is* a god's breath carries Lens stance, and the setting does not assert it.
2. **Lens content never enters the conflict queue against System.** A Lens that contradicts System is in-world error, and in-world error is content.
3. **In-world error is itself a phenomenon.** A widely held false belief needs a mechanism: ignorance, interest, a misleading regularity, enforcement. The Authorial Conceit "they believe X" is checked for grounding like any other Conceit.
4. **Lens content licenses nothing Authorial.** Belief does causal work through the fact of its being held, so the license runs from the Authorial Conceit that records the belief. The checker flags the leak (`stance-leak`).
5. **In-world documents default to Lens.** Chronicles, scriptures and in-universe books are Lens unless the corpus marks them Authorial. Declare the default at intake.
6. **Softness lives in Lenses.** A soft magic, an opaque politics or a mysterious cosmology is a fully specified System that its inhabitants, or the audience, have been shown little of. This is the Author Hardness Principle of Operative Mode §25.2, generalized to every Field.

## 1.6 Conceits, systems and Positions

A **Conceit** is any element of a setting at any tier: a System, a piece of Lore, a Narrative instance. Every Conceit is a Node of the Conceit Graph (`03-conceit-graph.md`), and every Conceit resides in some Domain of some Field.

**System and system.** Capitalized, *System* names the tier: a nomic statement. Lower case, *system* names a unit of the setting's ecosystem: a pan, a guild, a market, a species, a cult. Units are what interact, persist, adapt and emerge in the pipeline (`02-pipeline.md`). The graph records units through the Conceits that assert them, so relations and Generation Edges name Conceits. Wherever a sentence could be read either way, write *unit*.

**Positions.** An individual is not a Field. An individual is a particular (Narrative-tier, or System-tier under the Dominance Rule) that occupies a **Position**: the intersection of every Domain it belongs to, defined by its relations, meaning the levers it holds and the affordances it is subject to. The occupant is content. The Position is structure. A viewpoint character is analyzed as a Position made visible, never as a protagonist awaiting an arc.

## 1.7 Fields: the characteristic profile

Settings, like reality, are ordered into **Fields**: strata of emergence. Their characteristic profile:

**Metaphysics → Physics → Biology → Ecology → Mind → Culture → Technology → Economy → Polity**

- **Metaphysics:** what exists and what causation is: planes, the structure of time, magic substrates, the real divine. In a mundane setting it is the base setting's and carries no delta. In most fantasy it holds the deepest System.
- **Mind:** the laws of cognition, motivation and agency. A theory of preference and commitment (Axel's Claims Framework, for example) is a System of this Field.
- **Polity:** authority, coercion, law and war.

**Institutions** are not a Field. They are forms (System-tier rules and Lore-tier histories) occurring in Culture, Economy and Polity Domains. **Individuals** are not a Field either (1.6).

The profile is characteristic, not axiomatic. Each stratum's systems are characteristically the emergent products of persistent relations among systems of the strata beneath, and the ordering is the sediment of that iterated emergence, not a rule imposed on it. Settings may declare custom Fields. The Field axis runs orthogonal to the tier axis.

## 1.8 Domains

Within Fields are **Domains**, which are more specific and nest recursively: grasslands within geography; priesthood within religion within culture; knights within feudal warfare within warfare within polity.

**The Realized State.** A Domain's Realized State is a measurement of the systems within it taken as a collective: the Domain read as one object at coarse grain. Within a Domain, systems interact at least indirectly. Statements about a Realized State "interacting" re-describe constituent-level interactions at Domain grain; they never add an interaction to the ontology. **Realized States are measurements, not relata: no Realized State is ever itself engaged in an Interaction, and none is ever a sender.** Only units interact. The rule is load-bearing: were aggregates admitted as relata, they could enter Persistent Relations, pass the Persistence gate and become senders, and every Node would be co-generated by its own Domain's aggregate, which would make orphan detection trivial. In the graph, a measurement Conceit carries `"kind": "realized_state"`, and the checker bars it from relations and sender sets.

**The Domain Coherence Criterion.** A Domain is well formed iff its persistent-interaction graph is connected: every constituent unit reachable from every other through chains of Persistent Relations. Connectivity is what makes the Realized State one measurement rather than a heap of unrelated readings. An apparently single Domain whose interaction graph is disconnected is two Domains, or carries an unrecorded relation. Intra-Domain persistent contact is therefore constitutive of Domainhood, which grounds the co-resident priority of the witness sweep (V6).

**The System Coherence Criterion.** The same test, one level up. A setting's System base is one System iff its co-reference graph is connected, joining two Systems when they share a Domain, a defined term, a license, or a persistent Channel between their Domains. A disconnected base is several Systems sharing a setting. That is legitimate when declared, with the Couplings that join them recorded (Operative Mode §25.5 calls the shared layer a Metaphysic). Undeclared, it is a Wholeness finding (`04-structural-profile.md`).

## 1.9 Adjacency, Contact and Channels

Two Domains are **adjacent** iff units of the one interact with units of the other. Cross-Domain interaction is the qualifying feature of adjacency. The exchange channel is not infrastructure that precedes traffic: it is the traffic, described at Domain grain. Adjacency is selective, not universal (no Domain is adjacent to every other, and no Field to every other Field), and dynamic, forming and lapsing as interactions do.

Adjacency comes in two strengths.

- **Contact:** the Domains have interacted only temporarily. Contact qualifies adjacency and carries no configurational weight.
- **Channel:** the Domains interact persistently. Only a Channel carries Configuration (1.11).

Isolation is configurational silence: isolated Domains do not constrain one another. First contact is a Configuration event only once it opens a Channel.

Adjacency is primitive at Domain grain. **Field adjacency is derivative:** two Fields are adjacent iff at least one Domain of each shares a Channel.

**The Domain-to-Domain Biconditional.** The Domain-grain measurement of exchange between adjacent Domains A and B is nonzero iff at least one constituent-level Persistent Relation crosses the boundary. The constituent level is primitive and the Domain level is derived, exactly as Domain adjacency is primitive and Field adjacency derived. The checker tests both directions of the biconditional: a persistent relation over a Channel recorded as temporary, and a persistent Channel with no relation across it.

## 1.10 The Asymmetry Gradient

Adjacency is unequal. A Domain's adjacent Domains differ in the exchange their Channels can carry, and each Channel's two directions differ in capacity. Each Domain therefore has a **gradient** over its neighborhood: an ordering of its Channels by capacity, in each direction, recorded as `high`, `medium` or `low`. Constraint follows capacity: the greater the possible exchange from A into B, the more heavily A's Realized State configures B's possibility space.

**The classical ordering, demoted.** The Field profile survives as the **characteristic gradient profile**, the pattern that obtains when local gradients are aggregated across a typical setting: lower strata maintain high-capacity Channels into everything above and receive narrow ones in return. Constraint is bidirectional in principle; the profile describes its usual asymmetry, not its permitted direction. A setting may deviate. A cosmology in which belief shapes what exists runs a high-capacity Channel from Culture into Metaphysics. That is not a Constraint violation but a declared non-standard gradient, itself a System-tier Conceit (`"kind": "gradient"`) that enters the graph as a Node. The residual arrow no gradient can invert is temporal: the realized constrains the possible, never the reverse.

## 1.11 Configuration

The possibility space of every Domain is the joint product of the Realized States of its Channel-adjacent Domains, each weighted by its Channel's capacity toward the configured Domain. Every unit exists within the constraint space so generated, and on Realization contributes constraints back along every Channel its Domain has.

Ripples propagate along adjacency paths and attenuate with capacity. Configuration is local action compounding through the network, never action at a distance: the whole-setting reach of a Realized State is real but mediated, the transitive closure of adjacency, not a global field.

Configuration has a mechanism as well as a definition. It *is* the Domain-grain measurement of cross-boundary Persistent Interaction: constraint is not a force alongside interaction but interaction viewed at coarser resolution, as System is Narrative viewed at coarser resolution.

Recorded configuration restrictions ("given this chemistry, no large endotherms are possible") are System-tier Conceits with `"kind": "restriction"`.
