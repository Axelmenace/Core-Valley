# 03 The Conceit Graph: Doctrine, Record Format and Checker

**Contains:** the Interaction–Generation distinction; the three strata; the Arity, Coverage, Acyclicity, Adaptation-commit and Decomposition Rules; scope and the Persistence gate; peer links; roots, orphans, islands and grounding; critical load; the JSON record format, field by field; the checker's commands and finding list.

**Read when:** building or reading a Conceit Graph, recording a setting as JSON, running `scripts/conceit_graph.py`, or interpreting one of its findings.

---

## 3.1 The Interaction–Generation distinction

**Interaction** is the realization of causal relations between Realized units through exchange across a Channel; Interactions are temporary or persistent. **Generation** is the minting of a new unit. Only Generation is recorded as Edges. Interactions are recorded as **annotations** naming their participants, never as Edges. Temporary Interaction is diegetic causation and stays off the record entirely; Persistent Relations enter the annotation record; only Emergence from a gated Persistent Relation mints an Edge. Persistent Relations are how units are created and how one unit influences another, which is exactly why Edges, which record how units come to be, issue only from them.

## 3.2 The three strata

1. **License links:** the tier axis; the justification structure. A System licenses Lore; Lore licenses Narrative. Each link is Determined or Elected (`01-ontology.md` §1.3) and always bears both readings, Licensing and Inferential. Revision 04 called these Entailment links; Entailment is now the Determined case.
2. **Generation Edges:** the ecosystem axis; the genesis structure. One family, Emergent Generation, directed from a sender set to a receiver, minted only at the Persistence gate.
3. **Interaction annotations:** the dynamics record. Persistent Relations noted on Node sets, with participants and standing. Diegesis, where recordable at all, lives here.

Phase I mints Nodes; Phase II mints Edges. Recorded configuration restrictions and declared non-standard gradients are System-tier Conceits and enter as Nodes, connected by whatever exercises them.

## 3.3 Rules on Edges

**The Arity Rule.** Every Generation Edge has at least two senders. No unit is generated by a single unit: emergence issues from a Persistent Relation, and relation requires plurality. The rule constrains Edges, not Nodes: Stipulated roots legitimately have no incoming Edges.

**The load-bearing criterion.** A Conceit is enumerated as a sender iff its alteration would defeat the derivation. Background that could not defeat the Edge is geometry, not sender. **Configurers are not senders:** the Realized States of adjacent Domains shape a unit's possibility space but do not thereby generate it. Constraint is geometry; generation is Edges. This follows from the Realized-State prohibition: aggregates barred from relatahood are barred from senderhood.

**The Coverage Rule.** Every Generation Edge is itself licensed at System grain: the emergence of *that* receiver from *those* senders follows, defeasibly, from some governing System over the recorded Substrate, named in `covered_by`. An Edge no System covers is evidence of a missing axiom, not of a missing Edge form.

**The Acyclicity Rule.** Generation Edges form a directed acyclic graph. A unit cannot be among its own ancestors. Feedback is real, but it lives in Channels and loops (`04-structural-profile.md` §4.6), which are cyclic by nature; genesis is not. A genesis cycle almost always means an adapted state was not committed as a distinct Node.

**The Adaptation commit rule.** Uncommitted adaptation is annotation-level dynamics. An adapted state mints a new Node only when the author commits it as a distinct Conceit. The committing record is a Generation Edge whose senders are the unit's prior state and its Persistent Relation partners (Arity satisfied; the prior state is a distinct Node, so there is no self-loop), covered at System grain. Where the change is wholly rule-driven, it is instead a license under changed Substrate, with no new Edge.

**The Decomposition Rule.** Any apparent generation that resists the Arity or Coverage Rule decomposes into two or more links (Edges, or a license and an Edge) through intermediate Conceits. The decomposition is itself a finding: the intermediate Conceit was implicit content.

## 3.4 Scope: construction, not diegesis

The Conceit Graph records the setting's *construction*, at the outermost, authorial run of the pipeline. It does not record in-world causal history. The boundary is formal: **the Persistence gate**. A battle causing a retreat is a temporary exchange that never crosses the gate, and stays off the record.

**Peer links.** Apparent generation between peers of one tier resolves in exactly one of three ways:

1. **License under changed Substrate.** The prior peer alters the Substrate; the governing System, over the new Substrate, licenses the successor. Record the prior peer under `substrate` and the governing System under `from`.
2. **Emergence.** Peers in Persistent Relation jointly generate a receiver through a covered Generation Edge.
3. **Temporary interaction.** Diegesis, off the record.

Being forced to name the governing System in cases 1 and 2 is a feature: it surfaces the implicit System that Tier Mapping hunts for. The checker flags Lore licensed by Lore alone, and Narrative licensed by Narrative alone, until they are resolved. The one exemption is a Lens Conceit, whose genesis is the Authorial Conceit recording that the belief is held.

## 3.5 Roots, orphans, islands, grounding

**Roots** are Conceits with no incoming link. In a well-grounded setting the roots are exactly the Stipulations: axioms and substrate declarations. The checker reports the **root profile** (axioms, substrate, ungrounded) because it is the shortest true description of what a setting rests on.

**Orphans** are degree-zero Nodes over the union of License links and Generation Edges (coverage counts as a link for the covering System). Annotations confer no degree: a Conceit rich in recorded Interaction but absent from both the license and generation records is an orphan. Connectivity in diegesis is not connectivity in construction.

**Islands.** Orphan detection alone misses a cluster of Conceits linked only to one another. The checker therefore also computes connected components, and flags any component with no System in it: Lore and Narrative without structure, which is the Lore–System inversion made measurable. An orphan is an island of size one.

**Grounding** is support by fixed point. A Conceit is supported when it is Stipulated, or when some license has every source and substrate supported, or when some Generation Edge has every sender and its covering System supported. License records are conjunctive; several records for one target are alternatives. A Conceit that is linked but unsupported rests on an ungrounded root or on a support cycle (`ungrounded-chain`).

**Critical load.** Striking an axiom and recomputing support shows which Conceits lose every license: the axiom's critical load. Downstream reach counts everything it touches; critical load counts what depends on it alone. High-load axioms are where the setting's structure concentrates, and the witness sweep exercises them first. An axiom with no downstream reach is inert.

## 3.6 The record format

A Conceit Graph is one JSON object. Every list is optional; an empty setting is a valid file. The template is `assets/conceit-graph.template.json`; the worked example is `assets/examples/salt-marches.json`.

**Header**

| Key | Content |
|---|---|
| `setting` | Name of the setting |
| `revision`, `recorded` | Framework revision (`R05`) and date |
| `medium` | `codex`, `narrative` or `mixed` (`08-transmission.md`) |
| `seed` | List of Conceit ids stating the Seed (`04-structural-profile.md` §4.2) |
| `fields` | Custom Fields beyond the characteristic profile |

**Domains and Channels**

| Key | Fields |
|---|---|
| `domains` | `id`, `name`, `field`, `parent` (Domain id or null) |
| `channels` | `from`, `to` (Domain ids), `capacity` (`high`, `medium`, `low`), `persistent` (true for a Channel, false for Contact) |

**Conceits**

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `id` | Unique. Convention: `S` System, `L` Lore, `N` Narrative, `B` base or other substrate |
| `tier` | `SYS`, `LORE`, `NAR` |
| `domain` | Domain id |
| `text` | The statement |
| `role` | `axiom`, `substrate`, or null (derived) |
| `status` | `committed` (default), `witness`, `illustration` |
| `stance` | `authorial` (default) or `lens:<holder>` |
| `force` | SYS only: `strict`, `default`, `tendency` (`06-verification-protocol.md` F1) |
| `kind` | Optional: `law`, `prohibition`, `invariant`, `tendency`, `policy`, `restriction`, `gradient`, `realized_state` |
| `norm`, `deviation`, `movers` | Required when `force` is `tendency` (the Tendency Rule) |
| `table` | Required when `kind` is `policy` (the Discretion Table) |
| `terms` | Defined terms the statement uses, for Wholeness |

**Links and annotations**

| Key | Fields |
|---|---|
| `licenses` | `from` (list of source ids, conjunctive), `to`, `substrate` (list), `mode` (`determined` or `elected`) |
| `generations` | `id`, `senders` (two or more), `receiver`, `covered_by` (a System), `relation` (the Persistent Relation it issued from) |
| `relations` | `id`, `participants`, `standing` (`persistent`; `temporary` is off the record), `emergence` (Edge id) or `justification` (why none issues) |

**Structural Profile records**

| Key | Fields |
|---|---|
| `loops` | `id`, `domains` (the cycle, in order), `polarity` (`balancing`, `reinforcing`), `delay`, `limiter` (a System, required for reinforcing) |
| `dynamics` | `conceit`, `type` (`stability`, `recurrence`, `growth`), `supported_by` (loop id) or, for recurrence, `forced_by` (a periodic System) |
| `questions` | `id`, `kind` (`modal` or `actual`), `text`, `verdict`, `by` (warranting Conceits), and for an actual question `modal` (the modal question that covers it). Modal verdicts: `admitted`, `distributed`, `prohibited`. Actual verdicts: `determined`, `elected`, `open` |
| `taxonomies` | `id`, `parent`, `axes` (list of value lists), `cells` (`"a|b"` to a modal verdict), `exhaustive`, `exclusive` |
| `terms` | `term`, `defined_in` (Conceit id), `uses` (terms) |
| `primitives` | Terms deliberately left undefined |
| `codex_order` | Conceit ids in the order a codex presents them |

**Verification records**

| Key | Fields |
|---|---|
| `witnesses` | `id`, `class` (`standard`, `boundary`, `extremal`, `optimizer`, `long-run`, `magnitude`), `exercises` (Systems), `edges` (Generation Edges), `outcome` (`clean` or `conflict`), `ledger` (entry id when conflict) |
| `ledger` | `id`, `type` (`L`, `S`, `P`, `Q`), `severity` (P only: `P1` to `P3`), `status` (`open`, `closed`, `debt`, `accepted`), `outcome`, `rationale`, `debt_accepted`, `context_minted`, `yield`, `text` |
| `sweep` | `last_new_conflicts` (count from the latest full sweep), `fixed_point_sweeps` (consecutive sweeps minting none) |

## 3.7 The checker

`scripts/conceit_graph.py` needs only Python 3.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `check graph.json` | Every mechanical check: inventory, root profile, Structural Profile metrics, certification level and blockers, findings |
| `check graph.json --json` | The same as JSON, for further processing |
| `check graph.json --strict` | Exit 1 when any VIOLATION is found |
| `strike graph.json S4` | Perturbation: which Conceits lose every license if S4 is struck |
| `order graph.json` | Definitional order of terms for a codex; reports what is circular |
| `seal graph.json` | SHA-256 of the normalized graph, cited by a Completion Certificate |
| `dot graph.json` | Graphviz source: SYS boxes, Lore ellipses, Narrative text; Elected licenses dotted, substrate dashed, Edges bold |

**Findings.** VIOLATION breaks a framework rule and blocks certification at C0. GAP is a finding for the analysis. NOTE asks the analyst to look.

| Check | Level | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `identity`, `reference`, `tier`, `status`, `role`, `stance` | VIOLATION | The record is malformed |
| `direction` | VIOLATION | A license runs upward |
| `arity`, `coverage`, `self-generation`, `genesis-cycle` | VIOLATION | Edge rules broken |
| `grain` | VIOLATION | A Realized State used as relatum or sender |
| `adjacency` | VIOLATION | Interaction across non-adjacent Domains |
| `loop`, `channel`, `domain-nesting`, `license`, `relation`, `closure`, `taxonomy`, `dynamics`, `witness`, `ledger` | VIOLATION | Invalid record values |
| `ungrounded-root`, `ungrounded-chain`, `orphan`, `lore-system-inversion` | GAP | Grounding findings |
| `tier-skip`, `peer-license`, `stance-leak` | GAP | License findings |
| `persistence-gate`, `interaction-principle`, `domain-coherence` | GAP | Dynamics-record findings |
| `axiom-force`, `tendency-rule`, `discretion-point` | GAP | Systems that cannot be typed or closed |
| `closure-failure`, `product-test`, `partition` | GAP | Closure findings |
| `closure-warrant`, `sparsity-uncovered` | GAP or NOTE | A verdict without warrant; sparsity asserted without a covering modal verdict |
| `unbounded-loop`, `regulation-gap`, `loop-polarity` | GAP | Self-regulation findings |
| `undefined-term`, `circular-definition`, `forward-reference` | GAP | Codex findings |
| `wholeness`, `seed-remote`, `independence`, `inert-axiom`, `unanalyzed-cycle`, `biconditional`, `convergent-genesis`, `dual-genesis`, `field`, `root-role` | NOTE | Look and decide |

The checker decides what is mechanical. It does not decide whether a license is plausible, whether a partition is truly exhaustive, or how severe a strain is. Those are the analyst's, and the report says which findings need judgment.
