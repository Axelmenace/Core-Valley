# Worldbuilding Framework Analysis

## The Framework

**Worldbuilding** is defined as: the construction of systems, the deduction of recurring patterns from these systems, the generation of instances from those patterns, and the controlled transmission of all three to an audience with limited cognitive bandwidth.

---

### The Vertical Axis: Three Tiers

**I. Systembuilding** — *"Systems over time"*
- The underlying structure: long-term trends, constraints, probabilities
- Properties: Structural, Abstract, Causal
- Statement form: *"X happens"* / *"Y behave like this"* / *"Z produces these effects over time"*

**II. Lorebuilding** — *"Patterns within time"*
- Recurring patterns, histories, movements, cycles; what historians of the setting would write
- Properties: Institutional, Medium-term
- Content: Large events, conspiracies, movements, institutions, dynasties

**III. Narrativebuilding** — *"Instances in time"*
- Specific instantiations of Lore patterns: events, characters, individual histories
- Properties: Particular, Concrete, Experiential
- The only tier the audience ever directly contacts

**The Tier Relation: Entailment.** The tier axis records no Generation. A System, over its substrate, ontologically entails its Lore at all times; Lore entails its Narratives. Entailment is timeless — nothing new comes into being when a pattern is read at finer grain, because pattern and instance are one reality at two resolutions. What is *generated* on the tier axis is expression: statements, derivations, tellings — epistemic objects.

**Licensing** and **Inference** are the two readings of Entailment:
- **Licensing (top-down):** deriving expressions along the entailment — System licenses Lore, Lore licenses Narrative. Licensing is the Structuralist's operation: deriving content downward from fixed structure.
- **Inference (bottom-up):** abducing the entailing structure from its instances — Narrative infers Lore, Lore infers System. Inference is the Contentialist's operation, and the audience's only operation: recovering structure upward from vivid particulars.

**The Expression Rule.** Both readings always exist: every entailment is derivable along its direction and abducible against it. An entailed Conceit always follows plausibly from its entailing structure, and the structure is always in principle abducible from its instances. Whether an audience *registers* an Inferential reading is another matter entirely — see **Registration** below.

---

### The Field Axis: Fields, Domains, and Adjacency

A **Conceit** is any element of a setting at any tier — a System, a piece of Lore, or a Narrative instance.

Settings, like reality, are ordered into **Fields** — strata of emergence, characteristically running: **Physics → Biology → Ecology → Culture → Technology → Economy → Politics → Institutions → Individuals.** The ordering is a characteristic profile, not an axiom (see **The Asymmetry Gradient** below): each stratum's systems are characteristically the emergent products of persistent relations among systems of the strata beneath, and the classical hierarchy is the sediment of that iterated Emergence, not a rule imposed upon it. The Field hierarchy runs orthogonal to the System–Lore–Narrative axis: every Conceit, whatever its tier, resides in some Domain of some Field.

Within Fields are **Domains**, which are more specific and which nest recursively (Domains within Domains within Domains): grasslands within geography; priesthood within religion within culture; knights within feudal warfare within warfare within politics.

**The Realized State.** Each Domain's Realized State is a measurement of the systems within it taken as a collective — the Domain read as one object at coarse grain. Within a Domain, systems interact at least indirectly, through intermediaries; statements about a Realized State "interacting" are therefore re-descriptions of constituent-level interactions at Domain grain, never an additional interaction in the ontology. **Realized States are measurements, not relata: no Realized State is ever itself engaged in an Interaction with a Conceit.** Only systems are Interaction relata. This grain-hygiene rule is the Field-axis counterpart of the Dominance Rule, and it is load-bearing: were aggregates admitted as relata, they could enter Persistent Relations, pass the Persistence gate, and become senders — and every Node would be co-generated by its own Domain's aggregate, trivializing orphan detection.

**The Domain Coherence Criterion.** A Domain is well-formed iff its persistent-interaction graph is connected — every constituent system reachable from every other through chains of Persistent Relations. Connectivity is what makes the Realized State *one* measurement rather than a heap of unrelated readings. An apparently single Domain whose interaction graph is disconnected is in fact two Domains. This gives Domains a mechanical boundary test, and it grounds the Verification Protocol's co-resident priority (V6): intra-Domain persistent contact is not merely presumptive but constitutive of Domainhood.

**Adjacency.** Two Domains are adjacent iff systems of the one Interact with systems of the other: cross-Domain interaction is the qualifying feature of adjacency. The exchange channel is not infrastructure that precedes traffic; it is the traffic, described at Domain grain. Adjacency is selective, not universal — no Domain is adjacent to every other, and no Field to every other Field — and it is dynamic, forming and lapsing as interactions do. Isolation is configurational silence: genuinely isolated Domains do not constrain one another, and first contact is a Configuration event. Temporary interaction suffices to qualify adjacency; persistent interaction is what carries Configuration. Adjacency is primitive at Domain grain; **Field adjacency is derivative**: two Fields are adjacent iff at least one Domain of each shares a channel, the Field-level gradient being the aggregate of its Domains' channels.

**The Domain-to-Domain Biconditional.** The Realized States of adjacent Domains A and B stand in measurable Persistent Interaction iff at least one constituent-level Persistent Interaction crosses the boundary. The constituent level is primitive; the Domain level is derived — exactly as Domain adjacency is primitive and Field adjacency derivative.

**The Asymmetry Gradient.** Adjacency is unequal. For any Domain, its adjacent Domains differ in the degree of exchange possible across their respective channels, and a channel's two directions themselves differ in capacity. Each Domain therefore possesses a *gradient* over its adjacency neighborhood: a qualitative ordering of its channels by exchange capacity, in each direction. Constraint follows capacity — the greater the possible exchange from Domain A into Domain B, the more heavily A's realized state configures B's possibility space.

**The classical ordering, demoted.** The hierarchy of Fields is no longer axiomatic. It survives as the **characteristic gradient profile**: the pattern that obtains when local gradients are aggregated across a typical setting — physics maintains high-capacity channels into everything and receives narrow ones in return; individuals send narrowly and receive broadly. Constraint is bidirectional in principle; the classical ordering describes its usual asymmetry, not its permitted direction. Settings may deviate — a setting in which Culture configures Physics (a consensus-reality cosmology) is not a Constraint violation but a declared non-standard gradient, itself a System-tier Conceit entering the graph as a Node. The residual arrow that no gradient can invert is temporal: the realized constrains the possible, never the reverse.

**Configuration.** The possibility space of every Domain is the joint product of the realized states of its adjacent Domains, each weighted by its channel's capacity toward the configured Domain. Every system exists within the constraint space so generated, and, upon Realization, contributes constraints back along every channel its Domain possesses. Ripples propagate along adjacency paths, attenuating with channel capacity — configuration is local action compounding through the network, never action at a distance. The whole-ecosystem reach of a realized state is real but mediated: it is the transitive closure of adjacency, not a global field. Configuration has a mechanism, not merely a definition: it *is* the Domain-grain measurement of cross-boundary Persistent Interaction — constraint is not a force alongside interaction but interaction viewed at coarser resolution, exactly as System is Narrative viewed at coarser resolution.

### The Two-Phase Pipeline

The relation formerly carried by the single word "dependency" — and disambiguated in Revision 03 into Dependency, Selection, and Interaction — disambiguates fully into a ten-stage process in two phases. **Phase I encodes design** — composition by an author, a designer, an engineer; **Phase II encodes emergence** — genesis from the existing ecosystem of Fields, Domains within Fields, Domains within Domains, and systems within Domains. The two phases are genesis pathways funneling through one gate; Phase II is additionally the standing dynamical regime every Realized system inhabits, whatever its origin — designed systems join it at Interaction. The stage sequence is the canonical order of analysis, not a strict chronology: real dynamics interleave, and the loop nests recursively within Domains.

**Phase I — Design: Configuration → Discovery → Composition → Realization**

**Configuration** — of the possibility space of a Domain. The possibility space of every Domain is determined by the realized states of its adjacent Domains, as defined above: every system exists within the constraint space generated by the realized states of all adjacent Domains.

**Discovery** — of possibilities from within the possibility space of a Domain. Discovery is expansion of the *epistemic* possibility space, never the ontological one — the latter changes only when Configuration changes. Discovery is the theorization of systems within a Domain compliant with its constraint space. This gives intentional sparsity a formal address: Discovered-but-uncomposed space is sparsity as designed; un-Discovered space is the author's own frontier.

**Composition** — of candidate systems from discovered possibilities.

**Realization** — of those candidate systems compatible with the Domain's configuration. Realization operates under the **Constraint Principle**, which asks simply: *Can this candidate system exist within the said Domain's configuration?* Every candidate is either compatible or incompatible with the constraint space of its native Domain. Upon Realization a system gains **causal participation** within the ecosystem: it becomes constrained by its native Domain's constraint space, contributes constraints to the possibility spaces of adjacent Domains along every channel its Domain possesses, and becomes subject to their constraints in return.

**Retention — the standing condition.** Realization is entry; **Retention** is continuance. The Constraint Principle is applied not once but standingly, against a configuration that Reconfiguration keeps changing: a system Realized under one configuration can lose Retention when adjacent Realized States shift beneath it. Retention is not an eleventh stage — it is the permanent background against which the whole of Phase II runs, and Phase II's Selection stage is its special case applied to emergent intake. The split yields three fates:

- **Stillborn** — fails at Realization; never a Node; remains a theorized system in Discovery's epistemic space, still available as authorial material.
- **Transient** — Realized, later fails Retention; a full Node whose dissolution is diegetic — an annotation event, not an Edge. A transient's Generation Edges *stand* after its dissolution: if its Persistent Relations minted emergent systems before it dissolved, that genesis is not retracted — senders need not outlive receivers. Transients are prime committed-Lore material: collapse patterns are what historians of a setting write.
- **Retained** — Realized and continuing.

**Phase II — Emergence: Interaction → Persistence → Adaptation → Emergence → Selection → Reconfiguration**

**Interaction** — between systems. Interaction is the temporary realization of one or more causal relations between Realized systems through the exchange of matter, energy, information, authority, or other domain-specific quantities. Interaction takes place both within a single Domain and across Domains — but only across Domains adjacent to one another, cross-Domain interaction being the qualifying feature of adjacency itself.

**Persistence** — of interaction. All continued Interactions between a set of systems over time constitute a **Persistent Relation**. Persistence is a temporal property of repeated Interactions. The **Persistence gate** is the formal boundary between diegesis and construction (see the Graph, below): temporary Interaction stays off the record; Persistent Relations are recordable; only gated Relations feed Emergence.

**Adaptation** — of each constituent system in a Persistent Relation. Every system is modified by its Persistent Relations: systems modify their internal relations in response to external relations.

**Emergence** — of higher-order systems from Persistent Relations. Emergence is the formation of a new higher-order system from a Persistent Relation between existing systems — canonically within a Domain, but equally across adjacent ones; the emergent system Realizes into whichever Domain's constraint space admits it, which for stratified emergence is characteristically a higher stratum. Emergence is the sole minting operation for Generation Edges.

**Selection** — of adaptive systems from among emergent systems. Emergent systems are tested by the Constraint Principle and either dissolve or persist: incompatible with their native Domain's constraint space, they fail; compatible, they are Retained. Selection is Retention applied to the emergent intake stream — the same gate, the same operator.

**Reconfiguration** — of adjacent Domains by the resulting adaptive systems, which go on to constrain the possibility spaces of every Domain their native Domain is adjacent to. Reconfiguration is cycle closure, not a repeated stage: it hands adjacent Domains their new constraint spaces, and the pipeline re-runs from that point. What Revision 03 handled as a separate doctrine — feedback as iteration — is now built into the process: upward configuration is ordinary configuration along a channel, and nothing needs the special case.

**The economy of the pipeline.** Ten stages reduce to compact machinery: **one gate** (the Constraint Principle, applied to two intake streams — designed candidates at Realization, emergent systems at Selection — and applied standingly as Retention), **two genesis pathways** (design and emergence), **one dynamics loop** (Interaction–Persistence–Adaptation), and **one propagation operator** (Configuration, closing as Reconfiguration).

**Self-similarity of the design pathway.** Phase I is not the authorial pathway but the design pathway generally: an in-world engineer runs it diegetically; the author runs it at the outermost level. The framework is self-similar — the author is the designer whose Domain is the setting itself. Corollary for the graph: an in-world designer's Phase I run is *content*, not record; only the outermost, authorial run mints the construction record.

**The Generation Principle** *(successor to the Selection Principle)*: every Realized Conceit has a recorded genesis — designed (Phase I: admitted through the Constraint gate and entailed, at System grain, over its substrate) or emergent (Phase II: receiver of a Generation Edge from a Persistent Relation). A Conceit with no recorded genesis marks a detectable gap — an exposure gap if the genesis exists unstated, a structural gap if the ecosystem as recorded cannot yet produce it. A Conceit that no possible configuration could admit is not a gap but a conflict — a **Constraint violation** — and is intake for the Verification Protocol (V1).

**The Interaction Principle** *(restated mechanically)*: where systems stand in a recorded Persistent Relation, that Relation is a higher-order system in formation. A Persistent Relation annotation with no Generation Edge issuing from its constituents is a detectable gap.

### The Graph: Entailment, Generation, and Annotation

The relational vocabulary divides in two, and the graph stratifies into three records.

**The Interaction–Generation Distinction.** **Interaction** is the realization of causal relations between Realized systems through exchange across a channel; Interactions are temporary or persistent. **Generation** is the minting of a new system. Only Generation is recorded as Edges. Interactions are recorded as **annotations** on the participating Nodes — never as Edges. Temporary Interaction is diegetic causation and stays off the record entirely; Persistent Relations enter the annotation record; only Emergence from a gated Persistent Relation mints Edges. Persistent Relations are how systems are created and how one system influences another — which is precisely why Edges, which record how systems come to be, issue only from them.

**The three strata:**

1. **Entailment links** — the tier axis: the justification structure. A System entails its Lore; Lore entails its Narratives. Timeless, always bearing both readings (Licensing and Inferential).
2. **Generation Edges** — the ecosystem axis: the genesis structure. One family — **Emergent Generation** — directed from a sender set to a receiver, minted only at the Persistence gate.
3. **Interaction annotations** — the dynamics record: Persistent Relations noted on Node sets, with participants and standing. Diegesis, where recordable at all, lives here.

**The Arity Rule.** Every Generation Edge has at least two senders. No system is generated by a single system: emergence issues from a Persistent Relation, and relation requires plurality. (The rule constrains Edges, not Nodes: root Conceits — the author's axioms and free choices — legitimately have no incoming Edges and are senders only; the regress stops there.) Sender sets are bounded by the **load-bearing criterion**: a Conceit is enumerated as sender iff its alteration would defeat the derivation (F1's defeasibility, made structural). Background that could not defeat the Edge is geometry, not sender. In particular, **configurers are not senders**: the realized states of adjacent Domains shape a Conceit's possibility space but are not thereby its senders — constraint is geometry, generation is Edges. This follows *a fortiori* from the Realized-State prohibition: aggregates barred from relatahood are barred from senderhood.

**The Coverage Rule** *(successor to the Mediation Requirement)*: every Generation Edge must itself be entailed at System grain — the emergence of *that* receiver from *those* senders must follow, defeasibly, from some governing System over the recorded substrate. An Edge no System entails is evidence of a missing axiom, not a missing Edge form.

**The Adaptation commit rule.** Uncommitted adaptation is annotation-level dynamics. An adapted state mints a new Node only when the author commits it as a distinct Conceit: the committing record is a Generation Edge whose senders are the system's prior state and its Persistent Relation partners (Arity satisfied; prior state a distinct Node, so no self-loop), covered at System grain per the Coverage Rule — or, where the change is wholly rule-driven, entailment under changed substrate, with no new Edge.

**The Decomposition Rule.** Any apparent generation that resists the Arity Rule or the Coverage Rule decomposes into two or more links — Edges, or an entailment and an Edge — through intermediate Conceits. The decomposition is itself a finding: the intermediate Conceit was implicit content.

**Scope: construction, not diegesis.** The Conceit Graph records the setting's *construction* — at the outermost, authorial run of the pipeline. It does not record in-world causal history. The boundary is now formal rather than stipulated: **the Persistence gate**. A battle causing a retreat is a temporary exchange that never crosses the gate — off the record. Apparent peer-generation within a tier resolves in one of three ways:

1. **Entailment under changed substrate** — the prior peer alters the substrate; the governing System, over the new substrate, entails the successor. (What Revision 03 called mediated iteration, now literal: the "context" is the substrate the entailment runs over.)
2. **Emergence** — peers in Persistent Relation jointly generate a receiver through a Generation Edge, whose Coverage-Rule entailment names the governing System.
3. **Temporary interaction** — diegesis, off the record.

Being forced to state the governing System in cases (1) and (2) is a feature: it surfaces exactly the implicit System that Tier Mapping (Step 2) hunts for.

**The Conceit Graph.** Every Conceit is a Node. Phase I mints Nodes; Phase II mints Edges. Recorded configuration restrictions ("given this chemistry, no large endotherms are possible") and declared non-standard gradient profiles are themselves System-tier Conceits and enter the graph as Nodes, connected by whatever exercises them. **Orphans are degree-zero Nodes over the union of Entailment links and Generation Edges.** Annotations confer no degree: a Conceit rich in recorded Interaction but absent from both the entailment and generation records is an orphan — connectivity in diegesis is not connectivity in construction. Detection is mechanical: build the graph, find the degree-zero Nodes.

**Registration.** Whether an audience *registers* a link's Inferential reading is contextual. Registration is a function of the audience's state — accrued buy-in, exposure to repetition, adjacent pattern already registered — not a property of the link. One instance rarely produces registration; a recurring pattern characteristically does. Registration is therefore indexed to accumulated transmission exactly as derivations are indexed to substrate (F1): as the work proceeds and buy-in compounds, links that went unregistered at first exposure become registrable, and links registered early scaffold the registration of those above them.

### The Audience Asymmetry

Authors work top-down (System → Lore → Narrative); audiences experience bottom-up. Graph-theoretically: the author possesses the graph; the audience possesses only the Narrative-tier Nodes and must *earn* the rest, link by link, through registration. This means:

- System is simultaneously the foundation of the world and the element most distant from the audience.
- A System-tier Conceit is transmissible to the degree that a path of registrable links — entailments and Generation Edges — connects it to Narrative. "Consistently patterning System into Lore and instantiating into Narrative" is the operation of driving links above the registration threshold through repetition.
- The threshold itself is not fixed: it falls as buy-in accrues, which is the graph-theoretic content of the temporal exposition budget (below).
- A System Conceit **must be explicitly stated** exactly when no path to it can be driven above threshold within the work's budget — explicit statement is the purchase of registration at full exposition cost rather than its accrual through patterning.

---

### The Horizontal Axis: Transmissibility

Every setting begins from a **base setting** — a recognizable historical period or genre template — which functions as a compression algorithm. The audience already holds this information; only the **delta** (novel concepts introduced beyond the base) needs transmission. In pipeline terms, the base setting is the ambient substrate: the default configuration into which the setting's earliest derivations run.

Exposition cost is **combinatorial, not additive**: each novel concept interacts with every other domain in the setting, and those interactions must also be conveyed. Beyond a threshold, exposition burden overshadows Narrative itself.

**Audience Buy-In: the exposition budget.** The exposition threshold is not a constant of the medium but a function of **audience buy-in** — the attention-capital a work accrues through **resonance** (thematic, personal, emotional, creative, or otherwise), correlated with the willing suspension of disbelief. Delta determines exposition *cost*; buy-in determines exposition *budget*: a work can afford delta to the degree it has earned buy-in. Two consequences follow. First, buy-in is accrued over the course of the work, so the budget is temporal — works can (and characteristically do) open near the base setting and expand delta as resonance compounds. Second, exposition and resonance compete for the same finite attention: exposition spends what resonance earns, so delta spent ahead of accrued buy-in suppresses the accrual that would have funded it. The threshold failure above is therefore not a fixed ceiling but an overdraft.

In graph terms: buy-in sets the registration threshold, and the overdraft is links asserted before the scaffolding that would make them registrable has itself registered. Delta spent ahead of accrued buy-in is a path built top-down that the audience cannot yet climb bottom-up.

## Authorial Methodology: The Structuralist Position

This section governs all work on Axel's own projects and, where relevant, frames analysis of other settings. It refines the framework above with three commitments.

### 1. Worldbuilding as Art and Craft; Structuralist vs. Contentialist

Worldbuilding is equivalent to storytelling: both are simultaneously artform and craft, at different registers. The pantser/architect distinction in fiction is precisely mirrored in worldbuilding as the distinction between **content-first** and **structure-first** creators. "Structure" is the basic systems of the setting (the way everything works); "content" is the instances inhabiting those systems ("medieval political system" is structure; "the Kingdom of X" is content). Structure-first worldbuilders work deductively, instantiating content within a fixed system; content-first worldbuilders work abductively, inferring system from vivid particulars. The Structuralist's characteristic operation is **Licensing** (System → Lore → Narrative); the Contentialist's is **Inference** (Narrative → Lore → System). Under the graph these are literally the two readings of the same Entailment, traversed in opposite directions — which is why both modes of creation are valid in principle: the Inferential reading always exists alongside the Licensing one. These approaches demand different faculties — systems thinking versus creative intuition — and constitute genuinely different modes of creation. The craft register lives wherever structure lives; the art register lives in instantiation. Both registers are present in both temperaments; only the sequence differs.

**Axel is a Structuralist, not a Contentialist.** Structure is the primary object of the work. Missing content in Axel's projects is never a mistake or an overlooked feature — it is the project existing as intended. An unpopulated system is a finished artifact, not scaffolding.

### 2. Register Hierarchy: Systemsbuilding, then Lorebuilding, Never Narrativebuilding

The primary register of Axel's worldbuilding is **Systemsbuilding**; secondarily **Lorebuilding**. **Narrativebuilding is never the primary object of analysis.** Narrative may be *licensed* by System or Lore — derived downstream as demonstration, worked example, or sampled trajectory — but the analysis never lives at that tier. Content, when admitted, is load-bearing instantiation: it earns its place by what it reveals about or how it exercises the structure, not by how it enriches the world. Figures like a viewpoint character are treated as structural positions made visible (bundles of institutional levers and systemic affordances), not as protagonists awaiting arcs.

### 3. Tiers as Scales of Analysis, and the Dominance Rule

The tiers are not kinds of things but resolutions of one phenomenon. Any process can be analyzed at multiple scales: the personal, short-term, local, experiential scale is where Narrative resides; the impersonal, long-term, broad, near-abstract scale is where System resides; Lore sits between. Same process, different grain (cf. Braudel's longue durée / conjoncture / événementielle). Dynamic analysis — the system in motion, fault lines propagating, architecture evolving under stress — is Systemsbuilding; temporal extension does not change the grain. What tips analysis into Narrative is descent to the agent-experiential resolution.

**The Dominance Rule:** wherever a process or instance registers legibly across multiple scales along different dimensions (temporal, spatial, aggregative, consequential), the dimension in which it possesses System-tier scale takes priority and qualifies it as Systemsbuilding, even if it appears otherwise. Tier assignment is a maximum across dimensions, not an average. Consequences: a singular agent with system-scale duration and breadth (e.g., an institutional superintelligence) is Systems material; a brief, local event whose output is standing structure (a founding, a coup) is Systems material via the consequential dimension. Fine-grain rendering of any such process is available only as licensed illustration — a zoom-in for demonstration — never as the resident grain of analysis.

### Operational Consequences for Collaboration

- Never propose narrative development as a natural "next step," suggest an institution needs a story to justify it, or evaluate systemic elements by dramatic potential.
- Never treat sparse or absent content in Axel's projects as a gap to fill. When running Gap Detection (Step 6) on Axel's own settings, distinguish structural gaps (System/Lore genuinely underdeveloped) from intentional sparsity (unpopulated instantiation-space, which is the project as designed — Discovered-but-uncomposed space is sparsity by design). Only the former are findings.
- The criterion for any proposed addition is "does this illuminate or exercise the structure," not "does this enrich the world."
- Before treating any element, check its scale-profile across dimensions and conduct analysis at the highest grain it reaches.

## Analysis Protocol

Purpose: use this protocol to turn supplied setting material into a structured diagnosis. It moves from intake, to tier mapping, to coherence checks, to transmissibility, registration audit, and finally gap detection.

Use this protocol when given a setting to analyze. Input may be a wiki dump, prose document, outline, or a named setting Claude can draw on from general knowledge.

### Step 1 — Intake and Orientation
- Identify the base setting (historical period, genre template, or composite)
- List the delta: all novel concepts introduced beyond the base
- Note which tiers are legible in the material provided and which are absent or implicit

### Step 2 — Tier Mapping
Organize identified elements into the three tiers, and record each element's **Field and Domain** — the second coordinate is required to build the Conceit Graph. Record all three strata: **entailment links** (what entails what, at which grains), **Generation Edges** (direction, full sender enumeration under the load-bearing criterion, receiver, and the governing System per the Coverage Rule), and **Interaction annotations** (participants and standing — Persistent Relations only; temporary interaction stays off the record). Flag elements that are:
- **Misassigned**: a Narrative instance being asserted as a System rule, or a System rule stated as a Narrative fact
- **Orphaned**: a Conceit with no links in the Conceit Graph — degree-zero over the union of Entailment links and Generation Edges. Annotations confer no degree: a Conceit rich in recorded Interaction but absent from both the entailment and generation records is an orphan.
- **Implicit**: System rules inferable from Lore/Narrative but never explicitly stated by the author — including governing Systems surfaced by the Coverage Rule, the Decomposition Rule, or entailment under changed substrate

Present as a structured table or tiered list, not prose, for clarity.

### Step 3 — Coherence Audit
- **Entailment check** (downward licensing): Does each Lore element follow plausibly from at least one System statement? Does each Narrative instance follow plausibly from Lore? Each check is a defeasible derivation testing the recorded entailment over the recorded substrate.
- **Tier skipping**: Are Narrative elements licensed directly by System, bypassing Lore? This usually signals underdeveloped Lore.
- **Lore–System inversion**: Is the setting's System thin or absent, with heavy Lore that has no systemic foundation? Common in settings that are encyclopedic but structurally incoherent.
- **Constraint check**: Does each Conceit sit within the constraint space its Domain's adjacency neighborhood generates, evaluated to a declared neighborhood depth (high-capacity channels first)? Distinguish **hard Constraint violations** (no recorded configuration could admit the Conceit — a genuine conflict) from **Retention failures** (the Conceit cannot survive the *current* configuration — possibly deliberate content: a setting knowingly containing a doomed system is coherent, and the "violation" is its dissolution, on schedule).
- **Handoff**: apparent conflicts found here — including hard Constraint violations — are intake for the Verification Protocol (Step V1), which completes these local checks with joint, System-wide verification. Constraint violations typically type as L (near-strict incompatibility with the configuring realized states) or S (the configuring axiom was never written to govern that Domain).

### Step 4 — Transmissibility Assessment
- Base setting: Is it clearly established? Consistent?
- Delta inventory: List all novel concepts. Estimate the combinatorial interaction surface.
- Exposition burden: Is the burden proportionate to the narrative space in which it must be conveyed? Identify peak-burden zones.
- Compression opportunities: Which novel concepts could be anchored to base-setting analogues to reduce transmission cost?
- Threshold risk: Is the setting approaching the point where exposition burden overshadows Narrative? If so, which novel concepts are the primary drivers?

### Step 5 — Registration Audit (Audience Asymmetry Analysis)
Conducted **relative to a declared audience-state** — e.g., "at cold open," "by the midpoint, assuming the Lore patterning to that point has landed" — as the Verification Protocol certifies to a declared depth rather than absolutely.
- Which links' Inferential readings are registrable at the declared state? What can the audience reliably recover bottom-up (Narrative → Lore → System)?
- Which System Conceits have no path of registrable links to Narrative within the work's budget, and therefore require explicit statement (registration purchased at full exposition cost)?
- Are there System-level elements that will remain functionally invisible unless driven above the registration threshold through repeated Lore/Narrative patterning?
- Are there Lore elements that appear arbitrary because the System above them has not registered?

| Lore | ... | ... | ... |
| Narrative | ... | ... | ... |

For each gap, classify its kind:
- **Structural gap** — the tier, Field, or Domain is genuinely underdeveloped.
- **Exposure gap** — the content exists but hasn't been surfaced in the provided material.
- **Verification gap** — axioms or axiom-pairs never exercised by any derivation (added by the Verification Protocol); a finding even under intentional sparsity.
- **Generation gap** — a Realized Conceit with no recorded genesis (per the Generation Principle), or a Persistent Relation annotation with no Generation Edge issuing from its constituents (per the Interaction Principle). Boundary with intentional sparsity, per Discovery: an *empty* or merely theorized possibility space is intentional sparsity — Discovered-but-uncomposed instantiation-space, the project as designed; a *Realized* Conceit with no recorded genesis is a genuine finding. Unpopulated remains finished; unexplained remains the defect.

## Verification Protocol — Coherence as Hypothesis

Purpose: use this protocol when coherence itself must be tested rather than assumed. It extends the Analysis Protocol from local licensing checks into system-wide verification, conflict typing, patch testing, witness derivation, and certification to a declared depth.

This protocol operationalizes the framework's central promise: that a System can be *known* to be coherent, and that there is a point at which the knowing is done. It corrects three defects that surface when the framework's licensing relation is handled as classical entailment: the wrong logic (derivation is defeasible, not monotonic), a question-begging premise (axiom consistency assumed rather than tested), and a missing finish line ("consistent so far" is not consistent, and an unexercised System cannot be certified at all).

It extends Step 3 of the Analysis Protocol (Coherence Audit) from local, per-element checks to joint verification of the System as a whole. Its working object is the Conceit Graph assembled at Tier Mapping (Step 2): every patch, witness derivation, and revision below adds Nodes and links to that graph, and verification is the discipline of re-scanning it.

### Foundational Commitments

**F1 — Derivation is defeasible; entailment is total.** The framework's ontological claim is that a System, over its *complete* substrate, entails its Lore — entailment is total and timeless. But no derivation ever runs over the complete substrate: every derivation works from partial context, and is therefore defeasible — *follows plausibly*, not *is proven from*. Adding substrate can defeat a previously licensed conclusion without any axiom being retracted: a defeated license is not a defeated entailment but a derivation that mis-specified the substrate. Three consequences govern everything below:
- Two elements can each be validly derived from a consistent System and still conflict — colliding defaults are normal in non-monotonic derivation. "Non-contradictory axioms producing conflicting Lore" is therefore a legitimate phenomenon, not a paradox. It settles nothing about any individual case: the conflict may signal missing scope, missing priority, or genuine axiom inconsistency, and the protocol's job is to discriminate.
- No conflict between derived elements is permanent evidence of inconsistency, and no successful reconciliation is permanent proof of consistency.
- Every derivation is indexed to the substrate current at derivation. When the recorded substrate grows, prior derivations are re-checkable, not sacred.

**F2 — Consistency is the hypothesis, never the premise.** The consistency of a rich informal axiom set is not inspectable by reading the axioms; it is a conjecture that accrues support only through survived derivation. The ground of this commitment is now explicit: the total entailment structure — System over complete substrate — is never inspectable; it is probed only through defeasible derivations from partial context, which is why depth-D certification is the strongest claim the protocol can issue. The Verification Protocol is the epistemology of an inaccessible ontology. No audit may open from "the axioms are consistent, therefore the conflict must be contextual" — the conflict under examination is often the only evidence bearing on the question. The System's status is always cited as a verification depth (Step V7), never asserted flatly.

**F3 — Lore is the proof calculus.** Under the Structuralist Position, Lore is never the goal — but derivation is the only instrument by which coherence is exhibited: derivations are generated *expressions* testing entailment, which makes the proof-calculus reading literal. The tier therefore splits by role:
- **Committed Lore** — content the author adopts into the setting. Governed by the existing criterion: it earns its place by illuminating or exercising structure.
- **Witness Lore** — instrumental derivations run solely to exercise the System. A witness is scaffolding: its result is logged in the Ledger; the material itself may be kept, promoted to committed status, or discarded without loss.

Unpopulated remains a finished state. Unexercised is the defect. This converts "System-strong, Lore-starved" from an aesthetic remark into a measurable verification deficit, without touching intentional sparsity.

### The Conflict Typology

The unqualified word "contradiction" is retired. Every apparent conflict is typed at intake, because the three types demand different treatments:

**Type L — Logical conflict.** P and not-P strictly derivable from the axioms. Rare in natural-language Systems; most claimed L-conflicts are misclassified S or P. A *strict* L-derivation is maximally informative: it proves either a hidden premise (make it explicit — the conflict usually reclassifies) or genuine axiom-set inconsistency (Step V5).

**Type S — Scope collision.** Axioms conflict only when co-applied to a domain at least one was never written to govern. Cure: explicit scope or priority conditions. Cheapest reconciliation class — but scope-narrowing must still pass the Retraction Test, since shrinking an axiom's domain can be a retraction in disguise.

**Type P — Plausibility strain.** Jointly derived elements that strain the derivation without formal contradiction — e.g., a ruling class instituting its own demonization. Graded, theory-laden, resolved by plausibility arguments, not consistency proofs. Cure: causal-historical context that raises joint plausibility (the Sar Elay maneuver). Record severity at intake; a mild strain may be accepted as-is, which is not an option for Type L.

### Step V1 — Intake and Typing
Catalogue every apparent conflict, whether found by the Analysis Protocol's Coherence Audit (including hard Constraint violations from the Constraint check), encountered during generative work, or minted by a witness sweep (V6). For each: assign type (L/S/P), record the full derivation path of both conflicting branches as **substrate-indexed entailment chains and Generation Edges** (which axioms, over what substrate, through which links and Lore), and assign severity for P-types. Open a Ledger entry.

### Step V2 — Bounded Reconciliation Attempt
Attempt reconciliation strictly by added System-context: scope conditions, priority rules between colliding defaults, causal-historical chains, or explication of hidden premises. The attempt is bounded — default budget: three candidate patches per conflict per round. On failure, the entry is marked open, not resolved and not condemned: a failed search is evidence about the searcher, never a verdict on the System.

### Step V3 — Patch Validity Gates
A candidate patch that closes the conflict must pass two gates before the closure counts:

**Gate 1 — the Retraction Test.** Enumerate the consequences the affected axiom was originally introduced to license. The patch passes iff those consequences survive outside the conflict zone. A patch that strips the axiom's original extension is a revision wearing context's costume — reclassify it honestly as axiom surgery (V5) and book it as such.

**Gate 2 — the Fecundity Criterion.** The patch must independently license at least one new Lore pattern that (i) was not needed to close the conflict and (ii) is plausible on its own merits. Enrichment proves itself by excess content, not by mere closure.

Outcomes: passes both gates → **fecund reconciliation**; new System-context is banked and the entry closes. Passes Gate 1 but not Gate 2 → **ad hoc patch**: admitted provisionally, entered in the Debt Register, and scheduled for replacement or promotion. A debt patch that later demonstrates fecundity is promoted; one that accumulates dependents without ever showing fecundity is a degeneration signal (V5).

### Step V4 — Global Co-application Pass
Run once per round, over all patches minted in that round — never per patch. (1) Collect the round's patches. (2) Co-apply them pairwise on any shared domain. (3) Re-run the conflict scan over the enlarged System. Accounting rule: a conflict introduced by a patch is charged to the patch, not logged as a fresh discovery. Keep net-conflict accounting per round; a round that closes fewer conflicts than its patches open is degenerating, whatever it feels like locally. Local closure under jointly incompatible patches is the failure this step exists to catch.

### Step V5 — Escalation to Axiom Surgery
A conflict is ruled genuine — the System, not the searcher, is the problem — when any of the following holds:
- **Overdetermination**: the conflict re-derives through two or more independent axiom paths.
- **Degeneration**: successive rounds show negative net-conflict accounting, or the Debt Register grows without promotions.
- **Retraction exhaustion**: every patch that closes the conflict fails the Retraction Test.

Treatment: minimal axiom revision; re-run all derivations the revised axiom participated in; re-check every Ledger entry whose derivation path touched it. Log the revision as the highest-value finding class the protocol produces — a forced revision is the System correcting its own foundations, worth more than any quantity of successful enrichment.

### Step V6 — Witness Derivation Sweep
Verification is sought, not merely stumbled into. Systematically derive Lore-tier consequences to declared coverage targets:
- **Axiom coverage** — every [SYS] statement exercised in at least one witness derivation.
- **Interaction coverage** — axiom pairs co-exercised on shared domains, read off the annotation record. The interaction surface is combinatorial, exactly as exposition cost is; full pair coverage is rarely attainable, so declare a sweep depth and prioritize in two classes: **co-resident pairs first** (intra-Domain persistent contact is constitutive of Domainhood per the Domain Coherence Criterion — it needs no argument), then **cross-Domain pairs ordered by channel capacity** along the declared gradient, where persistent contact is recorded or derivable.
- **Generation coverage** *(optional target, declared as part of depth D)* — every Persistent Relation annotation examined for Emergence (its Generation Edge derived, or its absence justified), and every Generation Edge's System-grain entailment (the Coverage Rule) exercised by at least one witness derivation.

Tag witness material `[LORE] (witness)` with the standard genesis note. Every witness logs a path and an outcome: clean, or conflict → intake at V1. Witnesses are discardable after logging; the proof matters, the theorem's witness need not be kept.

### Step V7 — The Completion Certificate
A System is verified to depth D when, at declared parameters (coverage thresholds, including generation coverage if declared; patch budgets):
1. The conflict queue is empty — every intake entry ended in fecund reconciliation, promotion, surgery, or (for Type P only) explicit acceptance with recorded severity and rationale.
2. The Debt Register is empty, or every remaining item is explicitly accepted with a written rationale.
3. Coverage targets are met.
4. The most recent full sweep minted zero new conflicts — a fixed point at the current derivation depth.

Issue a dated certificate stating D and its parameters. The certificate is explicitly fallibilist: it is not a consistency proof — no such proof exists for a rich informal System — and any later derivation that mints a conflict reopens it. This is the finish line the bare phrase "internally consistent" fails to define: not consistency *simpliciter*, which is unattainable, but *exercised to depth D without unresolved conflict*, which is attainable, honest, and citable.

### The Reconciliation Ledger

One entry per conflict, maintained across rounds and settings:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| ID / Setting | Unique reference and home setting |
| Type | L / S / P (+ severity for P) |
| Derivation paths | Substrate-indexed entailment chains and Generation Edges of both branches |
| Attempts | Candidate patches tried, against budget |
| Outcome | Fecund reconciliation / debt patch / surgery / accepted (P only) / open |
| Context minted | The new System-context, stated as [SYS] |
| Fecundity yield | New Lore independently licensed by the patch |
| Charged conflicts | Conflicts this entry's patch introduced (V4) |
| Status date | Last action |

The Ledger serves three functions. It is the audit trail V4 and V5 accounting requires. It turns the generativity thesis into a measured quantity — fecund reconciliations over total conflicts is a ratio the corpus reports, not an article of faith. And it is the substrate for the extension below.

### Extension — Cross-Substrate Yield
Where the same [SYS] engine runs in multiple settings, the Ledger supports a comparative step: log where identical axioms produce **divergent** licensed Lore under different substrates, and where they produce identical Lore regardless of substrate. Divergence points are experimental yield — the substrate doing demonstrable causal work on the System's outputs. Identity across substrates means the substrate is decorative at that axiom. This step is optional per setting but is the only operation that converts a multi-setting corpus from one theorem restated in different notation into the repeated experiment it structurally resembles.

### Interfaces with the Rest of the Framework
- **Analysis Protocol, Step 3**: its findings — including hard Constraint violations — are V1 intake. Step 3 remains the local check; this protocol is its global completion.
- **Analysis Protocol, Step 6**: coverage results add gap kinds alongside structural and exposure gaps — the **verification gap** (axioms or axiom-pairs never exercised) and, where generation coverage is declared, unexamined Persistent Relations and unexercised Coverage-Rule entailments. Verification gaps are findings even under intentional sparsity, because they concern the System's evidential status, not its population.
- **Generative Mode**: genesis notes on generated elements double as witness logs when the element is tagged `(witness)`.
- **Structuralist Position**: untouched. Witness Lore is judged solely by whether it exercises structure, is never a content commitment, and may be discarded. Nothing here proposes population as a goal.

### Verification Principles
- Never assert or assume the System's consistency; cite its verification depth.
- The unqualified word "contradiction" is a category error; every conflict is typed.
- A failed reconciliation is an open conflict, never a verdict of inconsistency.
- Closure without fecundity is debt, not progress.
- Conflicts caused by a patch are charged to the patch.
- Witness Lore is instrumental and freely discardable; *unexercised System* is the only content-level defect this protocol names.
- Certificates are dated, parameterized, and fallibilist — a certificate reopens the moment a derivation conflicts, and that reopening is a finding, not an embarrassment.

## Generative Mode

Purpose: use this mode when creating or extending setting material. It keeps generation aligned with the framework by deriving downward from System where possible, inferring upward from Narrative where necessary, and labeling every output by tier.

When asked to develop a setting — whether from scratch or by extending existing material — work through the tiers deliberately.

### System First
1. Define the core System rules — the way everything works in this world and why
2. Identify the base setting and specify what differs at the System level
3. At each Domain (and across its adjacencies), run the pipeline's questions in order — **Phase I:** *What do the adjacent realized states permit?* (Configuration), *What can be theorized within that space?* (Discovery), *What candidate systems assemble from those possibilities?* (Composition), *Which candidates can exist under the current configuration?* (Realization); **Phase II:** *What exchanges occur?* (Interaction), *Which recur?* (Persistence), *How do the parties change?* (Adaptation), *What higher-order systems form?* (Emergence), *Which survive the gate?* (Selection), *What do the survivors constrain next door?* (Reconfiguration)
4. Derive Lore from System: what patterns, institutions, cycles, and movements does this System entail over time?
5. Derive Narrative suggestions from Lore: what specific events, characters, and histories would this Lore entail?

### Narrative First
1. Extract implicit System rules from the Narrative material — what must be true at the System level for these events to be plausible?
2. Construct the Lore that would license those Narratives
3. Make the System explicit and cite its verification status by depth, not by flat assertion

### Labeling Convention
When generating new content, tag each element by tier:
- `[SYS]` — System-level statement (structural, abstract, causal)
- `[LORE]` — Lore-level pattern (institutional, medium-term, historical)
- `[NAR]` — Narrative-level instance (particular, concrete, experiential)

Include brief **genesis notes** for each generated element: a one-sentence annotation stating its genesis — for designed content, the entailing structure and the substrate it runs over; for emergent content, the full sender enumeration of its Generation Edge and the governing System that covers it (the Coverage Rule). Every Generation Edge names at least two senders.

## Output Format

Purpose: use this section as the quick-reference map for response structure. It tells the user what shape the final answer should take for analysis, verification, generative, and mixed requests.

**For analysis requests:**
1. Setting Overview — base setting, delta summary, tier coverage of provided material
2. Tier Map — elements organized by tier with Field/Domain coordinates; entailment links, Generation Edges (with sender enumeration), and Persistent Relation annotations; orphaned/misassigned/implicit flags
3. Coherence Issues — tier skips, Lore–System inversions, hard Constraint violations and Retention failures
4. Transmissibility Assessment — exposition burden, compression opportunities, threshold risk
5. Registration Audit — registrable within budget vs. requiring explicit statement, indexed to the declared audience-state
6. Gap Table — structured by tier, gaps classified by kind (structural / exposure / verification / generation), with priority
7. Recommendations — ordered by structural importance

**For verification requests (Verification Protocol):**
1. Conflict Typology table — intake entries typed L/S/P, with severity for P
2. Reconciliation Report — per conflict: candidate patches and both gate results
3. Round Accounting — net conflicts closed/opened, Debt Register delta
4. Coverage Metrics — axiom, interaction, and (if declared) generation coverage against declared targets
5. Certificate Status — verified depth D, or the blocking items
6. Ledger Delta — entries opened, closed, promoted, or escalated this round

**For generative requests:**
1. Tier-labeled content using `[SYS]` / `[LORE]` / `[NAR]` tags
2. Genesis notes per element — entailing structure, or sender enumeration with governing System
3. Open questions — unresolved tensions or authorial decisions the framework cannot make

**For mixed requests (analyze + develop):**
Lead with the analysis (Steps 1–6), then transition to generative suggestions explicitly grounded in the identified gaps and coherence issues.

## Application Principles

- **Preserve tier distinctions rigorously**: Never allow a Narrative instance to pass as a System claim, or vice versa.
- **The Conceit Graph records construction, not diegetic causation**: The boundary is the Persistence gate. Temporary Interaction stays off the record; Persistent Relations are annotations; only Emergence mints Edges — and only the outermost, authorial pipeline run mints the record at all. An apparent generation with no System-grain entailment covering it is evidence of a missing axiom, not a missing Edge form.
- **Respect grain hygiene on both axes**: Tiers are resolutions of one phenomenon (the Dominance Rule governs assignment), and Realized States are measurements, never relata — Domain-grain talk of interaction is re-description of constituent-level interaction, not an additional relation.
- **Respect the authorial direction of travel**: Analysis should help the author see their world top-down even when the material arrives bottom-up.
- **Transmissibility is a constraint, not a defect**: High exposition burden is a cost to be managed, not always an error. Flag it neutrally. Registration is audited against a declared audience-state, never asserted absolutely.
- **Generative suggestions should preserve the author's System**: Do not introduce new System claims that contradict stated System — derive from it. Constraint checks run to a declared neighborhood depth, high-capacity channels first.
- **Apply the Dominance Rule before tier assignment**: An element's tier is fixed by the highest scale it reaches on any dimension, not by its surface appearance. Singular agents and datable events may still be Systems material.
- **For Axel's own projects, the Structuralist Position governs**: Systemsbuilding is the resident register, Narrative is never the primary object, absent content is intentional, and additions are judged by structural illumination rather than worldly enrichment.
