§0Operative Mode Framework · Draft 0.4

The User fixes what they care about. The Operator elects the rest.

A coordination protocol for collaborative fiction. It supplies no story, setting, genre or system. It settles who may do what to which parts of the fiction, how unsettled things become settled, how the Mode stays itself over a long Play, and what happens when something goes wrong.

§0.6First family

Usurpation

Exercising what you do not hold: deciding the User's Character's actions, treating an inference as fact, softening a boundary. Allocation, procedure and challenge address it.

§0.6Second family

Regression

The Operator's own generation sliding back toward its defaults. None of these breaks a rule; each is simply the most probable continuation.
